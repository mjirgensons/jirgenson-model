## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Revenue €18,000, costs €15,500, so profit is €2,500/month and margin is 2,500 / 18,000 = 13.9 %.
- Variable costs are €3,600, which is €30 per member (20 % of today's revenue). Fixed costs are 15,500 − 3,600 = €11,900.
- Contribution per member is €120 at €150. Break-even is 11,900 / 120 = 99.2, about 100 members, so today's position is about 21 members (17 %) above zero.
- **Price −20 % (€120), same 120 members.** There are two readings of the variable cost:
  - (a) Gold reading: variable cost stays at 20 % of revenue. Revenue 14,400, variable 2,880, profit −€380.
  - (b) Per-member reading: variable cost stays at €30 per member, as the task says ("vary with the number of members"). Revenue 14,400, variable 3,600, profit −€1,100.
  - Both responses use (b). That is a correct and arguably more literal reading of the task, and the conclusion is the same (a loss), so it counts under rule 2.
- **Members needed after the cut, reading (b).** Contribution is €90/member.
  - Break-even: 11,900 / 90 = 132.2, so 133 members.
  - Keeping today's €2,500: 14,400 / 90 = 160 members (+40, +33 %).
  - Getting revenue back to €18,000: 18,000 / 120 = 150 members.
- **25 % margin.**
  - At €150: 120N − 11,900 = 37.5N, so N = 144.2, rounded up to 145 members. Revenue €21,750, profit €5,500, margin 25.3 %.
  - At €120: 90N − 11,900 = 30N, so N = 198.3, rounded up to 199 members (+79, +66 %).
  - Under the gold's reading (a) the figure is about 180 members (+50 %). Either way, goals 3 and 4 cannot both hold without a large rise in members.
  - 25 % of today's €18,000 is €4,500, which is €2,000/month more than now.
- **Each extra €1,000/month of fixed cost:**
  - to hold today's profit: 1,000 / 120 = 8.3 members;
  - to hold a 25 % margin: 1,000 / 82.5 = 12 members at €150, and 1,000 / 60 = 17 members at €120.
- **Tax arrears.** 9,000 / 2,500 = 3.6 months of profit. Over 12 months that is €750/month, 30 % of today's profit.
- **R1's red line.** 105 members gives 105 × 120 − 11,900 = €700/month.
- **Reserve/cash:** not given.
