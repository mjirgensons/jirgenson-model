## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

From the task: revenue €18,000 from 120 members (€150 each); costs €15,500, of which €3,600 vary with the number of members, so fixed costs are €11,900.

- Current profit: 18,000 − 15,500 = €2,500/month. Margin 2,500 / 18,000 = 13.9 %.
- Variable cost per member: 3,600 / 120 = €30. Contribution at €150 is €120 per member. Break-even now: 11,900 / 120 = 99.2, so about 100 members. Safety margin: 2,500 / 120 = 20.8 members (about 17 %).
- Price −20 % gives €120. **Gold route** (variable cost as 20 % of revenue): revenue 14,400, variable 2,880, profit −€380. **Per-member route** (the task says the €3,600 "varies with the number of members", so €30 per member stays the same): revenue 14,400 − 11,900 − 3,600 = **−€1,100/month**. Both routes give a loss at the same member count. The per-member route is the more literal reading of the task, so I accept it as a correct alternative route (rule 2).
- At €120 under the per-member route: contribution €90. Break-even 11,900 / 90 = 132.2, so 133 members. Keeping €2,500 profit needs 14,400 / 90 = 160 members (+40, +33 %). Getting revenue back to €18,000 needs 150 members.
- A 25 % margin at €120: 90n − 11,900 = 0.25 × 120n → 60n = 11,900 → n = 198.3, so **199 members (+66 %)**. The gold route gives ≈ 180 (+50 %). Either way, goals 3 and 4 cannot both hold without a large rise in members.
- A 25 % margin at €150: 120n − 11,900 = 37.5n → n = 144.2, so 145 members. Revenue €21,750, profit €5,500, which is 25.3 %. A 25 % margin at 120 members and €150 means €4,500 profit, €2,000 more than now.
- Each extra €1,000 of fixed cost: 8.3 members to hold today's profit at €150; 12.1 members toward the 25 % margin at €150; 16.7 at €120.
- Tax arrears: 9,000 / 2,500 = 3.6 months of current profit. Over 12 months that is €750/month, which is 30 % of current profit. After the price cut there is no profit left to pay it from.
- Reserve/cash: not given.
