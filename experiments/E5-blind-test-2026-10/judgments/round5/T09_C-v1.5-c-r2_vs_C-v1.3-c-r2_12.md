# Judgment — T09 (Newspaper kiosk in a small town), pair 12

## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Turnover decline: 48,000 → 42,000 = −12.5 %; 42,000 → 37,000 = −11.9 %.
- 2026 run-rate: 25,000 / 9 × 12 = 33,333 (−9.9 % vs 2025). The decline runs at about 10–12 % a year over three periods. The town lost 25 % of its population (8,000 → 6,000).
- +30 % on 33,333 = 43,333, i.e. about +10,000. The 2023→2025 slide was 11,000, so +30 % would undo almost all of it while the town keeps shrinking.
- If nothing changes, 2027 ≈ 33,333 × 0.9 ≈ 30,000.
- 2025 costs = 37,000 − 6,000 = 31,000 (2,583 a month). Net margin = 6,000 / 37,000 = 16.2 %.
- 2026 profit before owner's pay: 2,333 if all costs are fixed, 5,405 if the margin holds at 16.2 %. 2027 at 30,000: −1,000 (all costs fixed) to 4,865 (margin held).
- Owner's hourly return: 6,000 / (55 × 48 = 2,640 h) = €2.27/h, or €2.10/h at 52 weeks.
- Weekly turnover: 33,333 / 52 = 641. 43,333 / 52 = 833. 700 × 52 = 36,400.
- Reserve: €4,000, about 1.55 months of total 2025 costs. No other liabilities are stated, so a wrong exit is cheap. A long lease renewal on falling turnover is the expensive mistake.

But it frames the town as an open question ("is the demand of the whole town falling, or your kiosk in particular?"). It never says the shrinking town is the strongest force, stronger than the owner's means. "Not only advertising" is conditional. The element is only partly present.
- M4 = 1. No marketing plan. It shows what +30 % takes: +10,000, almost the whole 11,000 lost in 2023–2025, in a town a quarter smaller, and 830 €/week against today's 640.
- M5 = 1. Lease decision point "in 5 months, earlier if … the notice period is longer". It sets the cost of each error side by side: a long renewal can mean rent for the full term and 55 h/week for −1,000 €, while leaving early costs 2,300–5,400 €/year but gives back 55 h/week. It concludes the long renewal is the dearer mistake. Options given: short renewal, orderly exit (sell off stock, hand over contracts), and a 12-week test capped at 300 €. Time is valued at ~2 €/h.
- M6 = 1. Nothing changes: 2027 ≈ 30,000, result −1,000 to 4,900, with both assumptions labelled. Test works: 700 €/week → 36,400. This is one conditional figure, not a range.
- M7 = 1. Measurable conditions: 700 €/week four weeks in a row means continue; a 12-week average under 640 € means plan an orderly exit; review in 4 weeks. Two questions, both relevant (rent and renewal length; monthly income needed to live). It does not ask what has been tried or what is growing in the town; instead it proposes an order of priorities for the owner to correct.
- M8 = 1. About 515 words.
