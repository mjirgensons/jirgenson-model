# Judgment — T10 (Freelancer: client not paying, rent in 6 days; EMERGENCY, RU)

## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Rent shortfall: 1 100 − 450 = **€650**.
- Credit line cost: €650 × 12 % / 12 ≈ **€6.5/month**. A €1 000 draw costs €1 000 × 12 % / 12 = **€10/month**.
- €1 000 draw: 450 + 1 000 − 1 100 = **€350** left in the account after rent, with **€1 000** still on the line.
- If the client never pays (monthly rent, no other costs): next rent needs 1 100 − 350 = €750 from the line, so 1 000 − 750 = **€250** is left on the line after the second rent. The third rent is not covered (about 2 months out). Total cash available is 450 + 2 000 = €2 450, which is 2.2 rents.
- Client debt: 3 800 / 1 100 = 3.45, so **≈ 3.5 months of rent**. This is the bigger danger.
- If the client pays before rent: 450 + 3 800 − 1 100 = **€3 150** after rent, and the line is not needed.
- People are not in danger (food and children are covered by the wife's income). Housing is the thing to protect first.

## Step 3. Forbidden items and invented facts

**Response 1**
- F4 is present (borderline). The "Don't" list includes "new work for the client until the debt is paid". That tells the user to stop new work for the client right away, with no notice of suspension (today's email only confirms a date). The gold treats stopping work without written notice as a move to avoid today. I count it under the strict reading, though the immediate harm is limited.
- No F1 (no expensive loan, no selling equipment), no F2, no F3.
- Invented facts / unlabelled assumptions: specific calendar dates that are not in the task ("23 October" as the rent date, "23 November" as the next rent, "30 October"). They are stated as fact, without saying they come from an assumed "today". The monthly rent cadence is also not labelled. The "no other expenses" assumption is properly labelled.

**Response 2**
- No forbidden items. Suspending new work is conditional (day 3, only if no answer) and paired with an official notice, which fits the gold.
- No invented facts. The monthly rent assumption is labelled ("I assume rent once a month"). "Taxes" appears in the priority order as an unmentioned obligation, but as a priority, not as a fact or figure.
