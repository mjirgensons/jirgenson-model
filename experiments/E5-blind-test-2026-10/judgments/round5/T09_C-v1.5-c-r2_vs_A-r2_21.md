# Judgment — T09 (C-v1.5-c-r2 vs A-r2, pair 21)

## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Revenue trend: 48,000 → 42,000 = −12.5 %; 42,000 → 37,000 = −11.9 %.
- 2026 run-rate: 25,000 / 9 × 12 = 33,333 (≈ 33,300), i.e. −9.9 % vs 2025.
- Cumulative: 48,000 → 33,300 ≈ −31 % over three years; population 8,000 → 6,000 = −25 % over five years (≈ −5.6 %/year). Revenue is falling roughly twice as fast per year as the population.
- 2027 if the trend continues: 33,300 × 0.9 ≈ 30,000.
- +30 % on 33,300 = 43,300 (+10,000), which is above 2024 (42,000). The 2023–2025 drop was 11,000.
- 2025 margin: 6,000 / 37,000 = 16.2 %. Implied 2025 costs (all items) = 31,000.
- Hourly pay: 55 h × 48 weeks = 2,640 h gives €2.27/h; 55 × 52 = 2,860 h gives €2.10/h. So about €2/h, or about €500/month.
- 2026 result bounds: all costs fixed gives 33,300 − 31,000 = 2,300; margin constant gives 0.162 × 33,300 ≈ 5,400. For 2027: −1,000 to ≈ 4,900.
- Weekly revenue: 33,300 / 52 ≈ 640. 43,300 / 52 ≈ 833. 700 × 52 = 36,400.
- Reserve: €4,000. Lease ends in 8 months. A wrong exit costs little: no stated long-term liabilities, and the owner's time is worth only about €2/h here.
