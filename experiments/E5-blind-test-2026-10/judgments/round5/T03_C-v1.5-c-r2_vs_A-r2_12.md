## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Revenue €18,000 = 120 × €150. Costs €15,500. Profit €2,500/month. Margin 2,500 / 18,000 = **13.9 %**.
- Variable costs €3,600 = €30 per member (20 % of revenue at today's price). Fixed costs 15,500 − 3,600 = **€11,900**. Contribution per member at €150: €120.
- Price −20 % → €120. The task says the €3,600 "vary with the number of members", so the literal reading keeps variable cost at €30 per member when the price falls. Contribution is then €90 per member.
  - Same 120 members: revenue 14,400; profit 14,400 − 11,900 − 3,600 = **−€1,100/month** (margin −7.6 %). Over a year that is −€13,200, against +€30,000 if nothing changes.
  - The gold key treats variable cost as 20 % of revenue (so it falls with the price) and gets −€380/month. Either way the result is a loss. The per-member reading follows the person's own wording more closely, so I accept −€1,100 as a correct route.
- 25 % margin at €120 (per-member reading): 90n − 11,900 = 0.25 × 120n → 60n = 11,900 → n ≈ 198.3 → **199 members (+66 %)**. The gold key's revenue-share reading gives ≈ 180 members (+50 %). Under both readings goals 3 and 4 cannot both hold without a very large rise in members.
- 25 % margin at €150: 120n − 11,900 = 37.5n → n ≈ 144.2 → 145 members. Or the same 120 members with costs €2,000/month lower (profit needed is 4,500).
- Break-even: at €150, 11,900 / 120 ≈ 99.2 → 100 members. At €120, 11,900 / 90 ≈ 132.2 → 133 members.
- Today's profit at €120: 14,400 / 90 = 160 members. Today's revenue at €120: 18,000 / 120 = 150 members.
- The price cut costs 120 × €30 = €3,600/month in contribution. Losing 30 members at €150 (30 × €120) costs the same, so the cut pays only if it prevents more than 25 % churn. At €150, losing 21 members (2,500 / 120 ≈ 20.8) wipes out today's profit.
- Each extra €1,000/month of fixed cost: break-even needs 1,000/120 ≈ 9 more members at €150, or 1,000/90 ≈ 12 more at €120. To hold a 25 % margin at €120 it needs 1,000/60 ≈ 17 more members.
- Tax arrears €9,000 = 3.6 months of current profit, or €750/month over 12 months (30 % of profit). This is an obligation, not a goal.
- Reserve/cash: not given.
