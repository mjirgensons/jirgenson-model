- Owner's hourly pay: 60 h × 4.33 ≈ 260 h/month; 1,800 / 260 ≈ **€6.92/h**.
- Reserve: 2,500 / 6,900 = **0.36 months** ≈ 11 days. Without owner pay: 2,500 / 5,100 = 0.49 months ≈ 15 days (≈ 2 weeks).

- Shock tests: 10 % revenue fall = €720 → −€420/month → reserve lasts 2,500 / 420 ≈ 6 months; two weeks closed ≈ 7,200 × 14 / 30.4 ≈ €3,300 of lost revenue > €2,500 reserve.
