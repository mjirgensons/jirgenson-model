## Step 1. Recompute the gold facts

- Cash: 22,000 → 13,000 = −9,000 over 6 months = **−1,500/month**.
- Receivables: 8,000 → 21,000 = **+13,000** (≈ +2,167/month).
- Paper profit: 6 × 3,000 = 18,000. Expected cash change after receivables: 18,000 − 13,000 = +5,000. Actual: −9,000. Gap = 5,000 − (−9,000) = **€14,000 unexplained** (≈ €2,333/month). The cause is unknown, so it has to be asked about.
- Reserve: 13,000 / 26,000 = **0.5 months** (about two weeks). That is red against a proposed one-month line.
- Months to zero at −1,500/month: 13,000 / 1,500 ≈ **8.7 months**.
- Big customer: 40% of the €21,000 receivables ≈ €8,400 at risk. Implied sales ≈ 26,000 + 3,000 = 29,000/month, so 40% ≈ €11,600/month of sales. On a P&L basis, losing that turns +3,000 into −8,600/month, and 13,000 / 8,600 ≈ 1.5 months. On a pure cash basis the runway is closer to about 1 month (1,500 + 11,600 burn). Either way the timeline gets much shorter.
