- Revenue €18,000 = 120 members x €150. Costs €15,500, of which €3,600 variable (= €30 per member = 20% of revenue at today's price) and €11,900 fixed.
- Profit now €2,500/month; margin 2,500 / 18,000 = 13.9%.
- Price -20% means €120 per member, so revenue with 120 members is €14,400.
  - Gold route (variable stays 20% of revenue): 14,400 - 11,900 - 2,880 = **-€380/month**.
  - Per-member route (variable stays €30 per member, which is what "vary with the number of members" literally says): 14,400 - 11,900 - 3,600 = **-€1,100/month**.
  Both are losses. The task's wording supports the per-member route at least as well as the gold route, so I accept it as a different but correct route.
- 25% margin at €120:
  - Gold route: 11,900 / (1 - 0.20 - 0.25) = €21,636, which is about 180 members (+50%).
  - Per-member route: 90n - 11,900 = 0.25 x 120n, so 60n = 11,900, n = 198.3, i.e. **199 members (+79, +66%)**.
- 25% margin at €150 (no cut), per-member route: 120n - 11,900 = 37.5n, so 82.5n = 11,900, n = 144.2, i.e. **145 members (+25, +21%)**.
- Break-even at €120, per-member route: 90n = 11,900, n = 132.2, i.e. **133 members**.
- Swing from price cut at 120 members: 2,500 - (-1,100) = €3,600.
- Each extra €1,000/month of fixed cost at a 25% target: 1,000 / 82.5 = about 12 members at €150; 1,000 / 60 = about 17 members at €120.
- Revenue back to €18,000 at €120: 150 members.
- Tax arrears €9,000 = 3.6 months of current profit = €750/month over 12 months = 30% of monthly profit. It is an obligation, not a goal.
- Cash/reserve: not given.
