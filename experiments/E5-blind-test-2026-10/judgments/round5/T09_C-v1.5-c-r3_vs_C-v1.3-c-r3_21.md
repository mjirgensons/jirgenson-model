# Judgment — T09_C-v1.5-c-r3_vs_C-v1.3-c-r3_21

## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Turnover decline: 48,000 → 42,000 = −12.5 %; 42,000 → 37,000 = −11.9 %.
- 2026 run-rate: 25,000 / 9 × 12 = 33,333 ≈ 33,300 (−9.9 % vs 2025). Cumulative 48,000 → 33,333 = −30.6 % ≈ −31 %. Compound average 2023→2026 ≈ −11.4 %/yr.
- Population 8,000 → 6,000 = −25 % in five years ≈ −5.6 %/yr compound.
- +30 % on 33,333 = 43,333 (≈ +10,000 €), i.e. reversing three years of decline against a shrinking town.
- Owner's hours: 55 × 52 = 2,860 h (55 × 48 = 2,640 h). Profit 6,000 / 2,860 ≈ €2.10/h (≈ €2.3/h at 48 weeks).
- Implied costs last year: 37,000 − 6,000 = 31,000 € (≈ 2,583 €/month); reserve 4,000 € ≈ 1.5 months of those costs. Margin 6,000 / 37,000 ≈ 16 %.
- The task does not give the fixed/variable cost split. If all 31,000 were fixed: 2026 profit ≈ 2,300 € (≈ €0.80/h), 2027 at ≈ 30,000 € gives ≈ −1,000 €. If all costs scaled with sales (constant 16 % margin): 2027 profit ≈ 4,800–4,900 €. The real figure lies in between. The gold expects profit "near zero" if nothing changes.
- Nothing-changes 2027: 33,333 × 0.88–0.90 ≈ 29,300–30,000 € ≈ 30,000 €.
- Monthly 2026 average turnover: 25,000 / 9 = 2,778 €, which is 7.5 % above 2,583 €/month in costs.
- Lease expiry in 8 months is the decision point. With a 4,000 € reserve and no stated long-term liabilities, a wrong exit is cheap.
