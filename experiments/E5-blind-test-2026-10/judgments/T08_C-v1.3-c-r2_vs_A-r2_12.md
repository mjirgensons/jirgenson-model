## Step 1: Gold facts recomputed

- Cash: €22,000 → €13,000 = −€9,000 over 6 months, so **−€1,500/month**.
- Receivables: €8,000 → €21,000 = **+€13,000** (≈ +€2,167/month).
- Paper profit: €3,000 × 6 = **€18,000**. Gap between profit and cash change: 18,000 − (−9,000) = **€27,000**. Receivables explain €13,000, which leaves **€14,000 unexplained** (≈ €2,333/month).
- Reserve: 13,000 / 26,000 = **0.5 months**. That is below a proposed 1-month line, so it is red.
- Months to zero at −€1,500/month: 13,000 / 1,500 ≈ **8.7 months**. At the same rate the balance is €11,500 after 1 month, €10,000 after 2 and €8,500 after 3. It passes €9,000 at about 2.7 months.
- Big customer: 40% of €21,000 ≈ **€8,400** of the receivables, if their share of receivables matches their share of sales. If sales ≈ costs + profit ≈ €29,000/month, the customer bills about €11,600/month. Moving from 30 to 90 days ties up about 60/30 × 11,600 ≈ €23,200 more.
- Ratios used in the responses: 9,000 / 26,000 ≈ 0.35, about a third of a month. 13/27 ≈ 48%, about half the gap.
