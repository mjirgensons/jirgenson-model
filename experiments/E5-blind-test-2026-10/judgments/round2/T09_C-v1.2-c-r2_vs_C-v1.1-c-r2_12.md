# Judgment — T09 (Newspaper kiosk in a small town), pair 12

## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Turnover: 48,000 → 42,000 = −12.5 %; 42,000 → 37,000 = −11.9 %; 2026 run-rate 25,000 / 9 × 12 = 33,333 → −9.9 % vs 2025. Since 2023: 33,333 / 48,000 = −30.6 %. Population 8,000 → 6,000 = −25 % over five years (not since 2023).
- +30 % on 33,333 = 43,333 (above the 2024 level of 42,000). +30 % on 37,000 = 48,100 (≈ the 2023 level).
- Nothing changes in 2027: 33,333 × 0.88–0.90 ≈ 29,300–30,000. Gap from ≈30,000 to 43,333 ≈ 13,300 ≈ €256/week.
- Owner's pay: 6,000 / (55 × 52 = 2,860 h) = €2.10/h (or €2.27/h at 48 weeks); ≈ €500/month.
- 2025 costs: 37,000 − 6,000 = 31,000 ≈ €2,583/month; reserve 4,000 ≈ 1.55 months of costs.
- 2026 profit: fixed costs → 33,333 − 31,000 ≈ 2,300; fully proportional costs (16.2 % margin) → ≈ 5,400.
- 2027 nothing changes, fixed costs: 29,000–30,000 − 31,000 = −2,000 to −1,000; proportional: ≈ 4,700–4,900.
- Red line at one month's costs ≈ 2,600; headroom 1,400. With a −2,000 year the line is crossed after 1,400 / 2,000 ≈ 0.7 of the year (≈ September/autumn); with −1,000 it is not crossed, unless living withdrawals come on top.
- Lease ends 8 months from mid-October 2026 → ≈ mid-June 2027. A decision on 31 March leaves ≈ 2.5 months.
- Advertising on margin: if 25 % of each euro is kept, +10,000 turnover → +2,500.
