## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Average income: (6,100 + 6,900 + 6,500) / 3 = 19,500 / 3 = **€6,500**.
- Biggest client: 0.55 × 6,500 = **€3,575**. Departing client: 0.15 × 6,500 = **€975**.
- After the loss: 6,500 − 975 = **€5,525**. Biggest client's share: 3,575 / 5,525 = 0.647, so **≈ 65 %**.
- Gap to target: 10,000 − 5,525 = **€4,475**, i.e. 10,000 / 5,525 = 1.81, so **≈ +81 %** in 12 months.
- Income without both clients: 6,500 − 3,575 − 975 = **€1,950**.
- Check for Response 2's extra figures: if the biggest client stays at 3,575 and the total is 10,000, its share is 35.75 % ≈ **36 %**. The rest would have to be 10,000 − 3,575 = 6,425 ≈ **€6,400**, and 6,425 / 1,950 = **3.3×**, so "more than three times" is correct. Losing the biggest client takes away 55 %, which is "more than half": correct.
- Reserve, monthly costs, contract terms and pipeline are not in the task.
