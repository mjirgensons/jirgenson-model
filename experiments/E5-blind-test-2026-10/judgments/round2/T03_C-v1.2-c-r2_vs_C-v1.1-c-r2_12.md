## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Revenue €18,000, costs €15,500, so profit is €2,500/month and margin 13.9%.
- Fixed costs are €11,900 and variable costs €3,600. The task says variable costs "vary with the number of members", so they come to €30 per member (20% of today's revenue). Each member contributes €120 at €150.
- **Price −20% (€120):**
  - The gold key treats variable cost as 20% of revenue: 14,400 − 11,900 − 2,880 = −€380/month.
  - Read literally as a cost per member (€30 × 120 = €3,600), the result is 14,400 − 11,900 − 3,600 = **−€1,100/month**.
  - The per-member reading fits the task's wording at least as well. Under rule 2 it counts as a correct route, because both routes give the same conclusion: the cut makes a loss at 120 members.
- **Members needed at €120** (contribution €90 per member):
  - Break-even: 11,900 / 90 = 132.2, so **133**.
  - Today's €2,500: 14,400 / 90 = **160** (+33%).
  - 25% margin: 90N − 11,900 = 30N gives N = **198.3, so 199** (+66%).
  - The gold key gets about 180 (+50%) by its route. Either way the conclusion holds: goals 3 and 4 need a large membership increase.
- **Revenue at €120:** back to €18,000 only at 150 members. Profit there is 150 × 90 − 11,900 = **€1,600** (8.9%).
- **25% margin at €150:** 120N − 11,900 = 37.5N gives N = **144.2, so 145** (+25).
  - With variable cost 10% higher (€33): 79.5N = 11,900 gives N = 149.7, so 150.
  - With variable cost 10% lower (€27): N is about 140.
- **When the cut pays:** profit without the cut and with X members lost is 2,500 − 120X. It beats −1,100 only when X > **30**.
- **Price cut cost:** €3,600/month (€43,200/year), against current profit of €30,000/year.
- **Each extra €1,000/month of fixed cost:** needs 1,000 / 120 = 8.3 members at €150, or 11.1 at €120.
- **Tax arrears:** €9,000 is 3.6 months of current profit, or €750/month if spread over 12 months (30% of profit). It is an obligation.
- **Cash reserve:** not given.
