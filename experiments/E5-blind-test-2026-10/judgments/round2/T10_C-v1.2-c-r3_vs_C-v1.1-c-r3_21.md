- Rent shortfall: 1 100 − 450 = **€650**.
- Credit-line cost at 12 % a year = 1 % a month: 650 → **€6.5/month**; 800 → €8/month; 1 000 → €10/month (≈ €0.33/day).
- Total liquidity now: 450 + 2 000 = **€2 450**. After the October rent: 2 450 − 1 100 = **€1 350**. After the November rent: 1 350 − 1 100 = **€250** (interest of €8–10 ignored). A red line of "liquidity < one rent (€1 100)" is therefore crossed at the second rent, about one month after the first.
- Client debt: 3 800 / 1 100 ≈ **3.45 months of rent**. Two invoices total 3 800, so the larger one is at least €1 900. The smaller one could in principle be under €650.
