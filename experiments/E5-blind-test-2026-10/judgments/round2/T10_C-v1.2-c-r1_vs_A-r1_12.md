## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Rent shortfall: 1 100 − 450 = **€650**.
- Credit line cost: 12 % a year ≈ 1 % a month. €650 × 1 % ≈ **€6.5/month**; €700 × 1 % = **€7/month**. Cheap, fast and reversible.
- If €700 is drawn and rent is paid: account 450 + 700 − 1 100 = **€50**; line left 2 000 − 700 = **€1 300**. That covers one more rent (1 300 − 1 100 = **€200** left).
- If the client pays €3 800 after that: 50 + 3 800 − 700 − ~7 ≈ **€3 143 ≈ €3 100**, about 2.8 months of rent.
- Client debt: 3 800 / 1 100 ≈ **3.45 ≈ 3.5 months of rent**. The bigger danger is that the client defaults or delays for a long time, not this month's rent.

- Budget: ≤300 words, ≤2 questions, first line = one action for today, Russian.
