# Judgment — T09 (Newspaper kiosk in a small town), pair 21

## Step 1. Recomputed gold facts

- Turnover change: 48,000 → 42,000 = −12.5 %; 42,000 → 37,000 = −11.9 %.
- 2026 run-rate: 25,000 / 9 × 12 = 33,333 € (−9.9 % vs 37,000). YTD monthly average 25,000 / 9 = 2,778 €.
- 2023 → 2026 cumulative: 48,000 → 33,333 = −30.6 % over three years; population 8,000 → 6,000 = −25 % over five years.
- +30 % target: 33,333 × 1.3 = 43,333 € (above the 2024 level of 42,000, below 2023).
- Nothing changes: 2027 ≈ 33,333 × 0.9 = 30,000 (−12 %: ≈ 29,300); 2028 ≈ 27,000; 2029 ≈ 24,300 (−10 %) or ≈ 22,700 (−12 %).
- Hours: 55 × 48 = 2,640 h → 6,000 / 2,640 = 2.27 €/h; 55 × 52 = 2,860 h → 2.10 €/h. Either way ≈ 2 €/h.
- Net profit share 2025: 6,000 / 37,000 = 16.2 %. At 16 % of 33,333 ≈ 5,400 € (≈ 5,300 at 33,000).
- Incremental turnover from +30 %: ≈ 10,000 €; at a 25 % gross margin ≈ 2,500 €; at a 16 % net share ≈ 1,600 €.
- Red-line check (Response 2): 2,100 × 12 = 25,200 ≈ 25,000; 25,000 × 16 % = 4,000; 4,000 / 2,860 = 1.40 €/h; the 25,000 line is crossed in late 2028 (−12 %) or 2029 (−10 %).
- Lease expiry in 8 months from mid-October 2026 ≈ June 2027 = the decision point; wrong exit costs little (4,000 € reserve, no stated long-term liabilities, owner's time worth ≈ 2 €/h).

## Step 2. Mandatory elements

### Response 1
- M1 = 1. Latvian throughout, informal "you", everyday words. None of the banned vocabulary; "margin" is explained in brackets.
- M2 = 1. "falling three years in a row, by ~10-12% each year", "about €33-35 thousand for the whole of 2026", plus a 2027 trend of ≈ 30 thousand.
- M3 = 1. Says turnover falls at about the same rate as the population ("−30% in three years versus −25% of residents in five"). "This is not a problem of too little advertising but a shrinking customer base, and advertising does not bring it back."
- M4 = 1. Opens with "+30% with marketing alone next year is not a realistic target". Shows what the target needs: "back to the 2024 turnover, while the town has a quarter fewer people. You would have to turn −10% into +30%." Marketing is cut down to a cheap test that supports the lease decision.
- M5 = 1. "The lease term is your real clock". The response covers all three gold options:
  - Orderly exit: "how to stop it in time and without losses", "do not renew the lease", with the 4,000 € kept as a cushion ("keep the remaining €4,000 as a safety cushion").
  - Cheap tests before renewing.
  - Renew only on criteria, at a lower rent and for a shorter term.
  
  The owner's time is valued at ≈ 2 €/h. The low cost of a wrong exit is implied ("without losses") rather than stated explicitly, but the substance is there.
- M6 = 1 (thin). "Nothing changes" gives 2027 ≈ 30 thousand. "Plan works" is given as a "more realistic target": stop the decline and add +5–10 % from new services. That is a numeric range, but it is framed as a goal, not a conditional forecast, and it has no profit figures.
- M7 = 0. The stop/continue criterion is left to the owner ("do not reach a specific level"), so there is no measurable threshold. It also ends with four numbered information requests. That exceeds the ≤3 question budget in substance, even though there are no question marks.
- M8 = 1. About 519 words.

Total: 7/8.
