## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Profit: 7,200 - 6,900 = €300/month, which is 300 / 7,200 = 4.2 % of revenue. A revenue dip of €300 (4.2 %) gives a loss month.
- Reserve: 2,500 / 6,900 = 0.36 months, about 11 days. Without the owner's pay, business costs are 5,100, and 2,500 / 5,100 = 0.49 months, about 2 weeks. Against any sensible line (one month = €6,900) this is red.
- Shock: a month with €2,800 less revenue gives a loss of 2,800 - 300 = €2,500, which empties the reserve.
- Owner's hourly pay: 60 h x 52 / 12 = 260 h/month. 1,800 / 260 = €6.92/h. Counting the profit, 2,100 / 260 = €8.08/h.
- No-shows: if the €7,200 is collected revenue, booked revenue is 7,200 / 0.8 = €9,000 and the no-show loss is €1,800/month. If half the slots are refilled, the loss is €900. If the €7,200 is the booked figure instead, collected revenue is 5,760 and the result is 5,760 - 6,900 = -€1,140/month.
- Gains from cutting no-shows (same bookings, same costs): at 15 % no-shows, served revenue is 7,650 (+€450). At 10 %, it is 8,100 (+€900).
- Reaching the line: the gap is 6,900 - 2,500 = €4,400.
  - At €300/month: 14.7 months, about 15.
  - At €750/month: 5.9 months.
  - At €1,200/month: 3.7 months, about 4.
  - So "plan works" means 4 to 6 months, which is about Feb to Apr 2027 from mid-October 2026.
- If nothing changes: reserve 2,500 + 900 = €3,400 after 3 months and 2,500 + 3,600 = €6,100 after 12 months.
- Price rises: 5 % of 7,200 = €360. 7 % = €504. Profit with no-shows at 10 % plus a 5 % rise: 300 + 900 + 360 ≈ €1,560, so "roughly €1,500" is fine.
- Dates: 17 Oct 2026 is a Saturday, so Friday 23 October is a valid date.
