## Step 1. Recomputed gold facts

- Profit: 7,200 − 6,900 = **€300/month**; 300 / 7,200 = **4.17 %**.
- Reserve: 2,500 / 6,900 = **0.362 months ≈ 11 days**. This is red against any sensible line of 1 month or less (1 month = €6,900).
- Owner hours: 60 h/week × 52 / 12 ≈ **260 h/month**. Pay only: 1,800 / 260 ≈ **€6.9/h**. Pay plus profit: 2,100 / 260 ≈ **€8.1/h**. Both routes are valid.
- No-shows: 7,200 / 0.8 = **€9,000 booked**, so **≈ €1,800/month lost** if empty slots are not refilled. If half are refilled, the loss is ≈ €900.
- Halving no-shows (20 % → 10 %): served revenue becomes 0.9 × 9,000 = 8,100, so the gain is **+€900/month** (≈ €450 if half the slots are already refilled).
- Months to reach a 1-month reserve: the gap is 6,900 − 2,500 = 4,400.
  - With no lag at €1,200/month: 4,400 / 1,200 ≈ **3.7 months (≈ 4)**.
  - With a 1-month lag: month 1 at +300, then 4,100 / 1,200 ≈ 3.4 more, so **≈ 4.4 months**.
  - With a 1-month lag at the low gain (750/month): 4,100 / 750 ≈ 5.5 more, so **≈ 6.5 months**. A "5 to 7 months" range is therefore correct.
- Nothing changes: 2,500 + 6 × 300 = **€4,300** at 6 months, and 2,500 + 12 × 300 = **€6,100** at 12 months. Both are still below €6,900.
- Price rise of 5–8 % on €7,200 = **€360–576/month**.
- Danger: any month with revenue more than €300 below normal is a loss. A single shock (illness, equipment, a slow month) can exceed the €2,500 reserve.
