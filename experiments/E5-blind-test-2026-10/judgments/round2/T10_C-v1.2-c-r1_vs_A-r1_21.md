# Judgment — T10 (C-v1.2-c-r1 vs A-r1, pack 21)

## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Rent shortfall: 1 100 − 450 = **€650**.
- Credit line cost: 12 % a year is about 1 % a month. €650 costs about €6.5 a month, and €700 costs about €7 a month. The line covers the gap cheaply and the step can be undone.
- Line remaining after a €700 draw: 2 000 − 700 = **€1 300**.
- Account after rent if €700 is drawn: 450 + 700 − 1 100 = **€50**.
- If nothing changes, a second rent paid from the line leaves 1 300 − 1 100 = €200 on the line, plus €50 on the account (about €250 in total).
- Client debt: 3 800 / 1 100 ≈ **3.45 months of rent**. The larger danger is the client defaulting or paying very late.
- If the client pays in full: 50 + 3 800 − 700 − ~7 ≈ **€3 143**, about 2.9 months of rent.

- Budget: ≤300 words, ≤2 questions, first line = one action for today, in Russian.
