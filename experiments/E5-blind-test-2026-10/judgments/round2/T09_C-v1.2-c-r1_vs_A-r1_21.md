# Judgment — T09 (C-v1.2-c-r1 vs A-r1, pack 21)

## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Year-on-year decline: 48,000 → 42,000 = −12.5 %; 42,000 → 37,000 = −11.9 %.
- 2026 run-rate: 25,000 / 9 × 12 = 33,333 € (≈ −9.9 % vs 2025). If December is stronger, 34–35k is plausible (−5…−8 %).
- +30 % on 33,333 = 43,333 € (on 34–35k: 44–45.5k). That is above the 2024 level (42,000), when the factory was already closed, and needs the decline to reverse in a town that lost about 25 % of its people (8,000 → 6,000).
- "Nothing changes" 2027: 33,333 × 0.88–0.90 ≈ 29,300–30,000 €.
- Owner's hourly return: 6,000 / (55 × 48 = 2,640 h) = 2.27 €/h; with 52 weeks (2,860 h) = 2.10 €/h; per month ≈ 500 €.
- 2025 costs = 37,000 − 6,000 = 31,000 €/yr ≈ 2,583 €/month; reserve 4,000 € ≈ 1.5 months of total outflow.
- 2027 profit if nothing changes at ≈ 29,500 € revenue: all costs fixed → 29,500 − 31,000 = −1,500 €; all costs variable (83.8 % ratio) → 29,500 × 16.2 % ≈ 4,780 €. So the profit range is ≈ −1,500…+4,800 €.
- Lease expires in 8 months, ≈ June 2027 (from October 2026). That is the decision point. A wrong exit costs little: the reserve stays intact, no long-term liabilities are stated, and 2 €/h is below almost any alternative use of 55 h/week.
