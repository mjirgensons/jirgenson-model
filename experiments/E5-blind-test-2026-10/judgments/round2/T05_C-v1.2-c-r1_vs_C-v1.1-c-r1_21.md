## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Profit: 7,200 − 6,900 = **€300/month**, which is 300 / 7,200 = **4.2 %** of revenue.
- Reserve: 2,500 / 6,900 = **0.362 months**, about **11 days** of expenses. That is red against any sensible line of ≤ 1 month (€6,900).
- Owner's hourly pay: 60 h × 52 / 12 ≈ 260 h/month, so 1,800 / 260 ≈ **€6.92/h**.
- No-shows: if €7,200 is revenue actually served, booked revenue = 7,200 / 0.8 = **€9,000**, so the loss is about **€1,800/month** (assumption: empty slots are not refilled). If half the slots are refilled, the loss is about **€900**.
- Halving no-shows (20 % → 10 %): served = 9,000 × 0.9 = 8,100, a gain of **+€900/month**. At 15 % no-shows the gain is +€450.
- Months to reach a 1-month reserve: the gap is 6,900 − 2,500 = €4,400.
  - Nothing changes: 4,400 / 300 ≈ **14.7 months**. The reserve is €3,400 after 3 months and €6,100 after 12.
  - Plan works at +€900 (profit €1,200): ≈ **3.7 months, about 4**. At +€450 (profit €750): ≈ 5.9 months.
  - With one month of ramp-up at €300: 2,500 + 300 + 2×750 = **€4,300** and 2,500 + 300 + 2×1,200 = **€5,200** at month 3. The reserve reaches €6,900 in ≈ 4.4 months (high end) to ≈ 6.5 months (low end).
- Shocks:
  - Revenue down by a third (−2,400) gives a hole of 2,400 − 300 = **€2,100**, which is **84 %** of the reserve.
  - Revenue down €2,800 gives a hole of €2,500, which is the whole reserve.
  - If €7,200 were the *booked* figure, collected revenue would be 5,760 and the salon would lose 5,760 − 6,900 = **−€1,140/month**.
- Price +5 %: 0.05 × 7,200 = **€360/month**.
- Date check: 17 Oct 2026 is a Saturday. Mon 19 Oct, Fri 23 Oct, Mon 16 Nov and Sat 31 Oct are all correct weekday/date pairs. Four to six months from now is mid-February to mid-April 2027.
