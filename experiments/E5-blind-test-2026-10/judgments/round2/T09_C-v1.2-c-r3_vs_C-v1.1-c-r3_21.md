## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- +30 % on 33,333 = 43,333 €, which is above the 2024 level (42,000). Achieving it means reversing three years of decline in a town that lost 25 % of its people (8,000 → 6,000).
- Profit margin 2025: 6,000 / 37,000 = 16.2 %. Profit at 2026 run-rate with the same margin ≈ 5,400 €.
- Hourly return: 55 h × 52 = 2,860 h → 6,000 / 2,860 ≈ 2.10 €/h (with 48 weeks, 2,640 h → 2.27 €/h). So roughly 2 €/h.
- Extra turnover from +30 % ≈ 10,000 €; at 16 % that is ≈ 1,600 € of extra profit; at 15–30 % it is 1,500–3,000 €.

- Red line 25,000 €/yr ≈ 2,100 €/month → ≈ 4,000 € profit at 16 % → 4,000 / 2,860 ≈ 1.40 €/h.
- Cutting 15 h/week × 52 = 780 h/yr.
- The lease ends ~June 2027, which is the decision point. The €4,000 reserve and no stated long-term liabilities mean the cost of a wrong exit is low. The owner's time is worth only about 2 €/h in the kiosk.
