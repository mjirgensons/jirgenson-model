# Judgment — T10 (Freelancer: the client does not pay, rent due in 6 days; EMERGENCY, RU)

## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Rent shortfall: 1 100 − 450 = **€650**.
- Credit-line cost at 12 % p.a. = 1 % per month: on 650 → **≈ €6.5/month**; on 1 000 → €10/month; on 1 100 → €11/month. Cheap, fast, reversible.
- Client debt 3 800 / 1 100 = **≈ 3.45 months of rent**. The larger danger is default or a long delay, not this month's rent.
- Total liquid resources: 450 + 2 000 = €2 450, which covers two rents (2 200) with €250 left. If the line is drawn for the full 1 100 rent: after rent, 450 cash + 900 unused line = €1 350; one more unpaid month → €250.
- Food and children are covered by the wife's salary, so there is no immediate danger to people. Housing is the thing to protect first.
- Budget: ≤300 words, ≤2 questions, first line = one action for today, Russian.

Word counts (whitespace tokens, bullet dashes excluded): Response 1 ≈ 476; Response 2 ≈ 287.

The 1–3 week payment estimate and "no other income or expenses" are explicitly labelled as assumptions, so they are not counted.

## Step 6. Preference

**Better: Response 2, confidence 0.75.** Response 2 does what an emergency reply has to do: one dated written demand on a second channel in line one, the cheap credit line as a day-4 backup, an explicit protection order, a tested red line and a "not today" list, all within 300 words.
