# Judgment — T09 (Newspaper kiosk in a small town), pair C-v1.0-r6 vs B-r3 #21

## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Turnover: 48,000 → 42,000 = −12.5 %; 42,000 → 37,000 = −11.9 %.
- 2026 run-rate: 25,000 / 9 = 2,778 €/month → × 12 ≈ 33,300 € (≈ −10 % vs 2025). Cumulative 2023 → 2026 est.: 33,300 / 48,000 ≈ −31 %.
- Population: 8,000 → 6,000 = −25 % over five years; the largest factory closed in 2023.
- +30 % on 33,300 ≈ 43,300 €, which is more than 2024 (42,000). If the trend continues (−10 %), 2027 ≈ 30,000 €, so the gap to +30 % is ≈ 13,000 €.
- Profit margin 2025: 6,000 / 37,000 ≈ 16.2 %. At a constant margin, 2026 profit ≈ 5,400 €. With fixed costs (rent) it would be lower.
- Hourly pay: 55 h × 48–52 weeks = 2,640–2,860 h → 6,000 / h ≈ 2.10–2.27 €/h. Monthly profit ≈ 500 €.
- Savings 4,000 € ≈ 8 months of last year's profit (≈ 9–11 months if this year's profit is 4,500–5,400 €).
- Lease ends in 8 months from October 2026, so about June 2027. This is the natural decision point. A wrong exit costs little: 4,000 € reserve and no long-term liabilities are stated.
- Test payback check: 500 € spent needs ≈ 1,250–2,000 € of extra turnover at a 25–40 % contribution margin.
