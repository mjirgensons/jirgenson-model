- Rent shortfall: 1,100 − 450 = **€650**.
- Credit-line cost: 650 × 12 % / 12 = **€6.5/month**. For other draw sizes: 800 → €8/month; 1,000 → €10/month. All trivial.
- Client debt 3,800 / 1,100 = **≈3.45 months of rent**; 3,800 / 450 ≈ 8.4× the current balance.
