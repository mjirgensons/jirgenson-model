## Step 1. Gold facts, recomputed

- 2023 → 2024: 48,000 → 42,000 = −6,000 / 48,000 = **−12.5 %**.
- 2024 → 2025: 42,000 → 37,000 = −5,000 / 42,000 = **−11.9 %**.
- 2026 run-rate: 25,000 / 9 × 12 = **33,333 ≈ 33,300**, which is 33,333 / 37,000 − 1 = **−9.9 % ≈ −10 %**.
- 2023 → 2026 cumulative: 33,333 / 48,000 = 0.694, so **≈ −31 %**.
- Population: 8,000 → 6,000 = −25 % over five years. Compounded, that is (0.75)^(1/5) = 0.944, so **≈ −5.6 % a year**. Turnover is falling about twice as fast per year (≈ −11 to −12 %).
- +30 % on 33,333 = **≈ 43,300**, which is above 2024's 42,000. It means reversing about three years of decline while the town keeps shrinking.
- 2027 if nothing changes: −10 to −12 % on 33,300 gives **≈ 29,300–30,000**.
- Pay per hour: 6,000 / (55 × 48 ≈ 2,640 h) = €2.27/h. With 52 weeks (2,860 h) it is €2.10/h. So **≈ €2–2.3/h**.
- 2025 net margin: 6,000 / 37,000 = 16.2 %. Implied total expenses: 31,000 a year, about 2,583 a month. The €4,000 reserve covers about 1.5 months of expenses.
- Lease: today is 2026-10-18, so 8 months ends around mid-June 2027. That is the natural decision point. A wrong exit costs little: the €4,000 reserve stays and the owner's time is only worth about €2/h.
- Date check for Response 2: 16 Nov 2026 and 14 Dec 2026 are both Mondays, so they are consistent with "every Monday".
