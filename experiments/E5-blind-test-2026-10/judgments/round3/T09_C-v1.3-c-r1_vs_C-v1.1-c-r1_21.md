# Judgment — T09 (Newspaper kiosk in a small town), pack 21

## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Turnover trend: 48,000 → 42,000 = −12.5 %; 42,000 → 37,000 = −11.9 %.
- 2026 run-rate: 25,000 / 9 × 12 = 33,333 ≈ 33,300; vs 37,000 = −9.9 % ≈ −10 %. Sustained decline over three periods; population 8,000 → 6,000 = −25 % in five years.
- +30 % on 33,333 = 43,333 ≈ 43,300 (above the 2024 level of 42,000, so reversing about two to three years of decline). The gap is ≈ +10,000 €/year, about 27 €/day at 365 days. If Q4 were stronger and 2026 ended at 36,000, then +30 % = 46,800 ≈ 47,000.
- Owner's hourly income: 6,000 / (55 × 48 = 2,640 h) = 2.27 €/h; at 52 weeks (2,860 h) = 2.10 €/h. In both cases ≈ 2 €/h. Per month: 6,000 / 12 = 500 €. The €4,000 reserve covers 4,000 / 500 = 8 months of that income.
- 2027 "nothing changes" (−10 % again): ≈ 30,000 (29,000–31,000). Profit: with costs proportional to turnover (margin 6,000 / 37,000 = 16.2 %), 30,000 × 0.162 ≈ 4,860. With fully fixed costs (37,000 − 6,000 = 31,000), 30,000 − 31,000 = −1,000. So the range is roughly −1,000 to +4,900, and the fixed-cost end is near zero or negative.
- Lease ends about 8 months after roughly mid-October 2026, so about mid-June 2027. That is the decision point. The cost of a wrong exit is low: about €2/h foregone, €4,000 intact, no stated long-term liabilities.
- Monthly threshold check: 2,500 €/month = 30,000 €/year, which equals the 2027 do-nothing forecast. The current 2026 average is 25,000 / 9 = 2,778 €/month.
