## Step 1 — Recomputed key numbers

- Revenue 2023→2024: 48,000 → 42,000 = −12.5 %. 2024→2025: 42,000 → 37,000 = −11.9 %.
- 2026 run-rate: 25,000 / 9 × 12 = 33,333 ≈ 33,300, i.e. −9.9 % vs 2025. Third straight year of decline, roughly 10–12 % a year.
- Population: 8,000 → 6,000 = −25 % over five years (≈ −5.6 % a year). Revenue is falling about twice as fast per year as the population.
- +30 % on ≈ 33,300 = ≈ 43,300, which is above the 2024 level of 42,000. On a 2027 "nothing changes" figure of ≈ 30,000 (33,300 × 0.9), the gap is ≈ 13,000–14,000.
- Profit 2025 ≈ 6,000 before owner's pay. Net margin 6,000 / 37,000 = 16.2 %. Hours: 55 × 48 = 2,640 h → €2.27/h; 55 × 52 = 2,860 h → €2.10/h. So ≈ €2–2.3 per hour, ≈ €500 a month.
- Expenses excluding owner's pay: (37,000 − 6,000) / 12 = 2,583 ≈ 2,600 a month. Reserve 4,000 = ≈ 1.5 months of that.
- Lease ends in 8 months (from mid-October 2026 → about mid-June 2027). This is the natural decision point. The cost of a wrong exit is low (€4,000 reserve, no stated long-term liabilities, owner earns ≈ €2/h).
- Response 1's own checks: 10,000 × 20–30 % = 2,000–3,000 ✓; 3,300 × 20–30 % = 660–990 ≈ "650–1,000" ✓; 26 Oct + 6 weeks = 7 Dec ✓; 4,000 − 300 = 3,700 ✓.
