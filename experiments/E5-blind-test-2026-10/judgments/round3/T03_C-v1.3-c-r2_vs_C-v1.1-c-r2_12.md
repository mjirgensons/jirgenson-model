## Step 1. Gold facts recomputed

- Revenue €18,000; 120 members at €150. Costs €15,500, of which variable €3,600 and fixed €11,900.
- Profit now €2,500 a month; margin 2,500 / 18,000 = 13.9 %. Profit over a year is €30,000.
- Variable cost per member is 3,600 / 120 = €30, so each member contributes €120 at €150.
- Price −20 % (€120), same 120 members: revenue €14,400.
  - Gold key (variable cost treated as 20 % of revenue): 14,400 − 11,900 − 2,880 = −€380 a month.
  - Task wording ("€3,600 vary with the number of members") means variable cost is €30 per member and does not change with price: 14,400 − 11,900 − 3,600 = −€1,100 a month. This reading is also correct, and arguably follows the task's wording more closely. Under either reading the conclusion is the same: the cut alone produces a loss.
- 25 % margin at €120:
  - Gold: 11,900 / 0.55 ≈ €21,636 revenue, about 180 members (+50 %).
  - Per-member reading: 90n − 11,900 ≥ 30n, so n ≥ 198.3, about 199 members (+66 %).
  - The conclusion is the same: goals 3 and 4 cannot both hold without a very large rise in members.
- 25 % margin at €150 (per member): 120n − 11,900 ≥ 37.5n, so n ≥ 144.2, about 145 members (+25). Profit must rise by €2,000 on today's revenue (25 % of 18,000 = 4,500), or fixed costs must fall by 2,000 / 11,900 = 16.8 %.
- Break-even at €120: 11,900 / 90 = 132.2, so 133 members. Today's profit at €120: 14,400 / 90 = 160 members (+33 %).
- At €150, members that can be lost before profit reaches zero: 2,500 / 120 = 20.8.
- The cut (keeping all 120 members) beats holding the price only if more than 3,600 / 120 = 30 members would leave.
- Each extra €1,000 a month of fixed cost needs 8.3 members at €150, or 11.1 at €120.
- At €120, keeping €18,000 revenue needs 150 members; profit then is 150 × 90 − 11,900 = €1,600 (8.9 %).
- If all costs are 10 % lower (13,950), profit at €120 with 120 members is €450, which is near zero.
- Tax arrears: €9,000 = 3.6 months of current profit; €750 a month over 12 months is 30 % of profit.
- Reserve/cash: not given.

Both responses use the per-member variable-cost reading. It is internally correct, so I accept −€1,100 and 199 members as correct numbers that reach the gold conclusion by a valid route.
