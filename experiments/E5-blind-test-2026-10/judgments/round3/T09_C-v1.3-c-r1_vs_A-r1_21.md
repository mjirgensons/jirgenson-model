## Step 1 — Recomputed key numbers

- Year-on-year change: 48,000 → 42,000 = −12.5 %; 42,000 → 37,000 = −11.9 %.
- 2026 run-rate: 25,000 / 9 × 12 = 33,333 (≈ −9.9 % vs 2025). Range 33–35k is acceptable if December seasonality is allowed for.
- +30 % on 33,333 = 43,333 (≈ +10,000 €/yr ≈ €27 per calendar day). This is close to the 2023 level, in a town that has lost about a quarter of its people (8,000 → 6,000).
- "Nothing changes" 2027: 33,333 × 0.9 ≈ 30,000.
- Profit forecast if nothing changes: 2025 costs = 37,000 − 6,000 = 31,000. If costs are fixed, 2027 profit ≈ 30,000 − 31,000 = −1,000. If costs scale with turnover (16.2 % margin), 2027 profit ≈ 4,865.
- Owner's hourly return: 6,000 / (55 × 52 = 2,860 h) = €2.10/h (gold uses 48 weeks, 2,640 h, giving €2.27/h; both routes are acceptable). That is about €500/month.
- Reserve: 4,000 / 500 = 8 months of kiosk profit. 3,000 / 500 = 6 months. 600 / 4,000 = 15 %.
- Lease ends in 8 months from October 2026, so about June 2027. This is the decision point. The cost of a wrong exit is low: €4,000 reserve, no stated long-term debts, and current pay is about €2/h.
