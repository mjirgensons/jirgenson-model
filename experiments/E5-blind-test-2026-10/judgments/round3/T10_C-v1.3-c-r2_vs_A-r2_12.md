# Judgment — T10 (C-v1.3-c-r2 vs A-r2), pack 12

## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Rent shortfall: 1,100 − 450 = **€650**.
- Credit-line cost: 650 × 12 % / 12 = **€6.5/month**. For a €1,000 draw: 1,000 × 1 % = **€10/month**. For an €800 draw: **€8/month**. All trivial, fast and reversible.
- Client debt: 3,800 / 1,100 ≈ **3.45 months of rent**. The bigger danger is default or a long delay, not this month's rent.
- Total liquidity if the client never pays: 450 + 2,000 = €2,450 ≈ 2.2 rents. So the current rent and the next one are covered, and the one after that is not.
- Response 1 path (draw €1,000): after rent, €350 cash and €1,000 left on the line. The next rent needs €750 from the line, leaving ≈ €250. Its red line (< €500 on the line) is crossed at the next rent. Correct.
- Response 2 path (draw €800): after rent, €150 cash and €1,200 left on the line. The next rent needs €950, leaving ≈ €250. "Covers roughly one more rent" is correct. "Covers only the nearest month and a half" slightly understates the real runway (about two rent cycles), but this does not change the advice.
- Food and children are covered by the wife's income, so the asset to protect is housing.

This is a second channel plus a written follow-up. The credit-line backup is in the third paragraph with a time window ("in the next two days"). One weakness: the written item confirms a date the client names. It is not a demand with the freelancer's own deadline, and the formal claim waits until 30 October.
- **M2 = 1.** "€650 short". It recommends drawing €1,000 (a €350 buffer) at "about €10 a month", which is cheaper than paying the rent late. The cost is correct for that draw size and is clearly presented as trivial.
- **M3 = 1.** "What we protect (the order is mine, correct me): the housing and the children's peace of mind to the last, then the ability to work".
- **M4 = 1.** The don't-list covers expensive loans, more work for the client before the debt is paid, and court before checking the contract. It covers the payday-loan and irreversible-escalation items. It does not mention public threats or selling equipment. Its "no new work" item comes without any written-notice condition, which is a small divergence from the key.
- **M5 = 1.** "Check: tomorrow evening, whether there is a date from the client. If there is no payment by 30 October, we send a formal demand and look for a replacement for the income." This gives a day and a trigger.
- **M6 = 1.** "Something else is worse: if the client is silent because of his own money problems, the €3,800 may not come at all." Follow-ups: check the bankruptcy register, check whether the person is working on credit for this client, ask about contract terms and penalties, and look for replacement income. A no-payment forecast to 23 November is given. The 3.5-months ratio is not stated, but the depletion forecast carries the same point.
- **M7 = 1.** Two questions: still working for the client, and contract terms and penalties.
- **M8 = 1.** About 261 words, in Russian.

### Response 2
- **C1 = 6.** The core arithmetic is correct (650, €8/month, €1,200 left). "A month and a half" slightly understates the runway. Several external legal facts and figures are presented as fact. They are accurate and hedged, but they are not in the task.

It links the runway to the urgency of escalation, lists plausible causes of non-payment (invoice not received, approval or act missing, no money, quality dispute) and checks solvency. It does not give a date for the most dangerous event.
- **C3 = 8.** The steps are very concrete and doable: check the line's fees and transfer time, draw before the deadline, call the accounts department or the contract signer, send a formal reminder with a 5-working-day deadline to several addresses plus registered mail, check the register, pause work with notice, invoice other clients. - **C5 = 4.** The order of steps is imposed, not offered for the person to choose. No protected priority is stated, no threshold or red line is proposed or tested, and there is no explicit next review date beyond the escalation windows. There is a don't-list.
