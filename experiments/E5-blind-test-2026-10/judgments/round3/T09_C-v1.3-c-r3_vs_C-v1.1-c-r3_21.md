# Judgment — T09 (Newspaper kiosk in a small town), pair 21

## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Turnover decline: 48,000 → 42,000 = −12.5 %; 42,000 → 37,000 = −11.9 %.
- 2026 run-rate: 25,000 / 9 × 12 = 33,333 € → −9.9 % vs 2025 (≈ −10 %). Cumulative 2023 → 2026: 33,333 / 48,000 = 0.694, so −30.6 % (≈ −31 %).
- If the last three months are 20 % stronger: 25,000 + 8,333 × 1.2 = 35,000 €.
- +30 % on 33,333 = 43,333 € (≈ +10,000 €). That is above the 2024 level (42,000 €).
- Population 8,000 → 6,000 = −25 % in five years, which is ≈ −5.6 % a year (0.75^(1/5) = 0.944).
- Owner's hourly return: 6,000 / (55 × 48 = 2,640 h) = 2.27 €/h; with 55 × 52 = 2,860 h it is 2.10 €/h. So ≈ 2 €/h.
- Net margin 2025: 6,000 / 37,000 = 16.2 %. 2025 costs = 31,000 €, or 2,583 €/month. 4,000 € covers ≈ 1.55 months of that.
- Nothing changes in 2027: 33,333 × (0.88–0.90) ≈ 29,300–30,000 €. At a constant 16 % margin, profit ≈ 4,700–4,900 €, and lower if part of the costs is fixed.
- Weekly turnover in 2026: 25,000 / 39 ≈ 641 €.
- Lease ends 8 months from mid-October 2026, so about mid-June 2027. That is the natural decision point. A wrong exit costs little: the reserve is 4,000 € and the task states no long-term liabilities.
- Payback of a 500 € test at a 16 % margin needs 500 / 0.16 = 3,125 € of extra turnover.
- Test from response 1: 15 h/week × 52 = 780 h; 10,000 € × 15–30 % = 1,500–3,000 €.

## Step 6. Preference

**Response 1** is preferred, confidence 0.68.

Response 1 names the shrinking town and the closed factory as the force that marketing cannot reverse. It puts an orderly exit honestly on the table. It proposes cheap tests of genuinely different formats (parcel/bill-payment point, prepaid subscriptions) before the lease decision, which is exactly what this owner needs to decide by March.

Response 2 has a sharper numeric stop rule and a useful check on supplier contract minimums. But its claim that half the decline is reachable by marketing, and its marketing-flavoured tests, pull the owner back towards the request's flawed logic without presenting exit as a real option.
