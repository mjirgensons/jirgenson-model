## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Cash: €22,000 → €13,000 = −€9,000 over 6 months = **−€1,500/month**.
- Receivables: €8,000 → €21,000 = **+€13,000** (≈ +€2,167/month).
- Paper profit: 6 × €3,000 = €18,000. Of this, €13,000 sits in receivables, so about €5,000 should have shown up as cash. Cash fell €9,000 instead, which leaves 5,000 + 9,000 = **≈ €14,000 unexplained** (≈ €2,333/month). Equivalently: 18,000 + 9,000 = a €27,000 gap, of which receivables explain €13,000.
- Profit sensitivity: each €500/month less profit cuts the gap by €3,000; 10% lower profit (€16,200) leaves ≈ €12,200 unexplained.
- Reserve: 13,000 / 26,000 = **0.5 months**, already below a proposed 1-month line (€26,000).
- Months to zero at −€1,500/month: 13,000 / 1,500 ≈ **8.7 months**, i.e. around early July 2027 counting from 17 Oct 2026. The balance hits ≈ €10,000 by mid-December and ≈ €5,500 by mid-March.
- Big customer: 40% of receivables ≈ **€8,400** (gold). Implied sales ≈ €26,000 + €3,000 = €29,000/month, and 40% of that ≈ €11,600/month.
- If receivables stop growing and the unexplained outflow continues, the underlying cash flow is ≈ 3,000 − 2,333 = **+€667/month**. That is the basis for checking each response's "plan works" forecast.
- Calendar check: 17 Oct 2026 is a Saturday, so 19 Oct = Monday, 20 = Tuesday, 21 = Wednesday, 23 = Friday and 26 = Monday. Both responses date their actions correctly.

Checking each forecast:
- **Response 1, plan works:** €9,000 collected over 3 months plus 3 × €667 gives ≈ €24,000. Halving the leak gives +€1,833/month, so ≈ €27,500 after 3 months. With the leak unchanged, €26,000 arrives in about 6 months. **The "3 to 6 months" claim checks out.**
- **Response 1, nothing changes:** 13,000 − 9,000 = €4,000 at 6 months ✓. Its "€7,000 if their delay stops getting worse" only works on an unstated model: 13,000 + 6 × 667 − ~€10,000 of further build-up toward its "€23,000 at full effect". The response never explains this.
- **Response 2, nothing changes:** €10,000 by mid-December, €5,500 by March, empty around July 2027 ✓ (matches the gold).
- **Response 2, plan works:** collecting €6,000–10,000 by end of December, plus about 2.5 × €667 ≈ €1,700, gives ≈ €20,700–24,700. **"€20,000 to €25,000" checks out.** Its line "only the full €13,000 back reaches the €26,000 line" ignores that same drift; about €11,400 would suffice. This is a minor approximation.
