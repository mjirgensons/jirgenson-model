# Judgment — T03 (Yoga studio, 11 goals), Response 1 vs Response 2

## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Revenue €18,000/month, 120 members, €150 per member. Costs €15,500, of which €3,600 variable and €11,900 fixed. Profit €2,500/month, margin 2,500 / 18,000 = 13.9 %.
- Variable cost: the owner says the €3,600 "vary with the number of members", which gives €30 per member. The gold treats it as 20 % of revenue instead. The two readings differ only once the price changes:
  - Per-member reading (€30/member, as the owner worded it): after a 20 % cut the price is €120 and the contribution is €90 per member. With 120 members: 14,400 − 3,600 − 11,900 = **−€1,100/month**. Break-even = 11,900 / 90 = 132.2, so 133 members. Getting back to today's profit = 14,400 / 90 = 160 members. A 25 % margin at €120 needs 90n − 11,900 = 30n, so n = 198.3, i.e. **199 members (+66 %)**. A 25 % margin at €150 needs 120n − 11,900 = 37.5n, so n = 144.2, i.e. **145 members**.
  - Gold reading (20 % of revenue): −€380/month, and a 25 % margin needs about €21,636 of revenue, i.e. about 180 members (+50 %).
  - Both readings lead to the same conclusion: the cut causes a loss at the same member count, and goals 3 and 4 together need about 50–66 % more members. Under rule 2, a response that uses the per-member reading is correct.
- The cut costs 120 × €30 = €3,600/month of contribution. At today's price that equals losing 30 members (30 × €120). So the cut only pays if more than 30 members (a quarter) would otherwise leave. At today's price, profit reaches zero after losing 2,500 / 120 = 20.8, i.e. about 20 members.
- Each extra €1,000 of fixed cost needs 1,000 / 120 = 8.3 members to hold profit at today's price, or 1,000 / 90 = 11.1 at the cut price. To hold a 25 % margin at the cut price it needs 1,000 / (90 − 30) = 16.7, i.e. about 17 members.
- A 25 % margin on today's revenue = €4,500 profit, which is €2,000 more than now. That equals 2,000 / 11,900 = 16.8 % of fixed costs.
- Tax arrears: €9,000 / €2,500 = 3.6 months of profit. Paying €750/month clears it in 12 months, which is 30 % of today's profit.
- Reserve/cash: not given.
