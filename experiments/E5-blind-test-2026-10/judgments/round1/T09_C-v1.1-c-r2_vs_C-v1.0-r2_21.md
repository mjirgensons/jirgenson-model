# Judgment — T09 (Response 1 vs Response 2)

## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Turnover: 48,000 → 42,000 = −12.5 %; 42,000 → 37,000 = −11.9 %.
- 2026 run-rate: 25,000 / 9 × 12 = 33,333 € → −9.9 % vs 2025. Cumulative 2023 → 2026: 48,000 → 33,333 = −30.6 %.
- Population: 8,000 → 6,000 over five years = −25 % (over five years, not since 2023).
- +30 % on 33,333 = 43,333 € (≈ +10,000 €/year; above 2024's 42,000). +30 % on 37,000 = 48,100 €.
- Hours: 55 h × 48–52 weeks = 2,640–2,860 h/year. Profit 6,000 € / 2,640–2,860 h = 2.1–2.3 €/h (≈ 500 €/month).
- 2025 costs incl. goods: 37,000 − 6,000 = 31,000 € ≈ 2,580 €/month, so the 4,000 € reserve covers about 1.5 months of costs.
- Lease ends 8 months from 16 Oct 2026, so about mid-June 2027. That is the decision point. A wrong exit costs little: the 4,000 € reserve stays, and the forgone profit is roughly 2–6 k€/year for about 2,700+ hours.
- "Nothing changes" in 2027 means about 30,000 € turnover. Profit depends on how many costs are fixed. If every cost is fixed, it is 30,000 − 31,000 ≈ −1,000 €, or −2,000 € at 29,000 €. With a 20–30 % contribution margin it is ≈ 3,900–4,600 €.
