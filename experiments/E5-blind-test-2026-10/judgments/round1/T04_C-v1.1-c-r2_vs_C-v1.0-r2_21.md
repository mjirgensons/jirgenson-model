## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Three months: 6,100 + 6,900 + 6,500 = 19,500, so the average is **€6,500/month**.
- Biggest client: 0.55 × 6,500 = **€3,575**. Second client: 0.15 × 6,500 = **€975**.
- After the loss: 6,500 − 975 = **€5,525**. Biggest client's new share: 3,575 / 5,525 = 64.7%, so **≈65%**.
- Sensitivity: if the biggest client's share is 50% or 60% instead of 55%, its new share is 3,250/5,525 = 58.8% or 3,900/5,525 = 70.6%, so roughly 59–71%.
