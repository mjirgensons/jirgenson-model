## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Average income: (6,100 + 6,900 + 6,500) / 3 = 19,500 / 3 = **€6,500**.
- Biggest client: 0.55 × 6,500 = **€3,575**. Second client: 0.15 × 6,500 = **€975**. All others: 6,500 − 3,575 − 975 = **€1,950**.
- After the second client goes: 6,500 − 975 = **€5,525**. Biggest client's share: 3,575 / 5,525 = 0.647, about **65%**.
- Gap to €10,000: 10,000 − 5,525 = **€4,475**, which is **+81%** (10,000 / 5,525 = 1.81). Spread over 12 months that is about €373 a month.
- If the biggest client stays at €3,575 and income reaches €10,000, their share is **35.75%, about 36%**.
- If the biggest client also leaves: about **€1,950 a month**.
- Not given: reserve, monthly costs, contract terms, pipeline.

Word counts were done by hand. They include headings and exclude list numerals, and both responses were counted the same way. Response 1 is about **712 words**. Response 2 is about **506 words**: 493 of running text plus 13 words of headings.

Question marks addressed to the person: Response 1 has **5**, one per numbered question. Response 2 has **6**, because numbered question 2 holds two separate questions: "...how much is in the account?" and "Is the €10,000 revenue, or what is left for you after costs?".
