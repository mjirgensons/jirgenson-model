- Calendar check: 16 Oct 2026 is a Friday, so 2 Nov 2026 is a Monday (correct in Response 1), and 30 Oct is two weeks out (correct in Response 2).
- A wrong exit is cheap: at most 8 months of profit at about €2/h (≈ 3,000), with no stated long-term liabilities. A wrong long renewal can eat the €4,000.
