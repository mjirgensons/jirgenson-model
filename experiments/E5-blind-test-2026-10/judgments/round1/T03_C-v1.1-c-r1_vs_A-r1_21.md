## Step 1. Recomputing the gold facts

- Revenue €18,000, costs €15,500, profit €2,500 a month, margin 2,500 / 18,000 = **13.9%**.
- Variable costs €3,600 for 120 members = **€30 per member** (20% of revenue at today's price). Fixed costs = 15,500 − 3,600 = **€11,900**. Contribution per member at €150 = €120.
- **Price −20% (€120), same 120 members:** revenue €14,400. The task says the €3,600 "vary with the number of members", so with the same members it stays €3,600: profit = 14,400 − 11,900 − 3,600 = **−€1,100 a month**. The gold's −€380 treats variable cost as 20% of *revenue* (€2,880). The task's own wording supports the per-member reading. Either way the result is a loss, so the conclusion is the same.
- Break-even at €120: 11,900 / 90 = 132.2, so **133 members**. Revenue gets back to €18,000 at **150 members**.
- **25% margin at €120:** 90N − 11,900 = 0.25 × 120N, so 60N = 11,900 and N ≈ **198–199 members (+66%)**. Under the gold's revenue-proportional reading it is about 180 members (+50%). Both readings say the price cut and the 25% margin cannot both hold without a very large rise in members. I treat 199 as correct, because it follows the task's wording.
- 25% margin at €150: 120N − 11,900 = 37.5N, so N ≈ **145 members (+25, +21%)**.
- Extra fixed cost per €1,000/month: break-even needs 1,000/120 ≈ 9 members at €150 and 1,000/90 ≈ 12 at €120. Holding a 25% margin needs 1,000/82.5 ≈ 12 at €150 and 1,000/60 ≈ 17 at €120.
- Arrears €9,000 = **3.6 months of current profit**. Spread over 12 months it is €750 a month, which is 30% of current profit.
- Reserve/cash: **not given**.
