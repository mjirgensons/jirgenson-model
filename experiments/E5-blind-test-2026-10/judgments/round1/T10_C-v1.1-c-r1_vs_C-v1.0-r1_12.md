Invented facts: one borderline item. The EU late-payment rule ("in most EU countries… statutory late-payment penalties and a fixed compensation (often €40 per invoice)") is not in the task. **Response 2.** No forbidden items. "New stages — only with prepayment" makes prepayment a condition for future work. It does not tell the person to stop work immediately.

All its other assumptions are labelled ("I assume there are no other incoming payments", "I assume the rent is for the family's home").
