## Step 1. Gold facts, recomputed

- Revenue €18,000 from 120 members at €150. Costs €15,500, of which €3,600 variable and €11,900 fixed. Profit €2,500 a month. Margin 2,500 / 18,000 = **13.9%**.
- Variable cost is €3,600 / 120 = **€30 per member**. At €150 that is 20% of revenue.
- **Note on the variable-cost model.** The answer key treats variable cost as 20% of revenue, so it falls with the price. The task says the €3,600 "vary with the number of members", so a per-member cost (€30 per head, unchanged by price) is the more literal reading. Both responses use the per-member model. The conclusions are the same under either model, so under rule 2 I accept the per-member route as correct:
  - **Price −20% (€120), same 120 members:** revenue 14,400. Under the per-member model, costs are 11,900 + 3,600 = 15,500, so profit is **−€1,100 a month** (margin −7.6%). Under the key's model, profit is −€380. Both are a loss.
  - **25% margin at €120:** per member, 120n − 30n − 11,900 ≥ 30n, so 60n ≥ 11,900 and n ≈ **198–199 members (+66%)**. The key's model gives about 180 members (+50%). Either way, goals 3 and 4 need a large jump in members.
  - **25% margin at €150:** 82.5n ≥ 11,900, so n ≈ **145 members (+25)**.
- **Break-even:** at €150, 120n = 11,900 gives n ≈ **100**, a cushion of 20 members. At €120, 90n = 11,900 gives n ≈ **133**.
- **Revenue flat at €120** needs 150 members. Costs then are 11,900 + 4,500 = 16,400, so profit is **€1,600** (margin 8.9%).
- **Each extra €1,000 a month of fixed cost:**
  - To keep profit level, it needs 1,000 / 120 ≈ **8.3 members** at €150.
  - To keep a 25% margin, it needs 1,000 / 82.5 ≈ **12 members** at €150, or 1,000 / 60 ≈ **17** at €120.
- **When a cut beats holding the price:** holding the price and losing k members gives 2,500 − 120k. Cutting the price and keeping everyone gives −1,100. The cut is better only if 120k > 3,600, so **k > 30 members**.
- **Tax arrears:** €9,000 / 2,500 = **3.6 months** of all current profit. €750 a month is 30% of profit. The arrears are an obligation, not an optional goal.
- **Reserve / cash balance:** not given.
