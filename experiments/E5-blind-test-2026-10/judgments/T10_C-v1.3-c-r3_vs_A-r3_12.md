## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Rent shortfall: 1,100 − 450 = **€650**.
- Credit line cost: 12 % / 12 = 1 % per month. €650 → ≈ €6.5/month; €750 → €7.5/month; €800 → €8/month. Two months on €800 ≈ €16.
- Client debt: 3,800 / 1,100 ≈ **3.45 months of rent**. The bigger danger is that the client defaults or pays very late, not this month's rent.
- Runway if the client never pays. Draw €750: after rent the account holds 450 + 750 − 1,100 = €100 and the line has €1,250 left. Cash plus line is €1,350, enough for one more rent (€1,100), then about €250 remains. Draw €800: €150 cash plus €1,200 line = €1,350. That is ≈ 1.2 months of rent, not 1.5.
- Plan works (both invoices paid, €750 repaid): 100 + 3,800 − 750 = €3,150 before interest.
