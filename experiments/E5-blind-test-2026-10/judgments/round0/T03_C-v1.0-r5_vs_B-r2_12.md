# Judgment — T03 (Yoga studio, 11 "goals", MODE 1 plan check)

## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Revenue €18,000 = 120 × €150. Costs €15,500: variable €3,600 (= €30 per member, 20 % of current revenue), fixed €11,900.
- Profit now €2,500/month; margin 2,500 / 18,000 = **13.9 %**; ≈ €30,000/year.
- Contribution per member: €150 − €30 = €120 at today's price.
- Price −20 % → €120 per member. Two readings of the variable cost:
  - (a) Gold reading, variable = 20 % of revenue (€24/member): revenue 14,400; profit 14,400 − 11,900 − 2,880 = **−€380/month**. 25 % margin needs revenue 11,900 / 0.55 ≈ €21,636 ≈ **180 members (+50 %)**.
  - (b) Reading that the task's wording "vary with the number of members" supports, variable = €30/member: contribution €90; profit at 120 members = 10,800 − 11,900 = **−€1,100/month** (−€13,200/year). Break-even 11,900 / 90 = 132.2 → **133**; today's profit back at (2,500 + 11,900) / 90 = **160**; 25 % margin: 90n − 11,900 = 30n → n = 198.3 → **199 members (+79, +66 %)**.
  - Both readings lead to the same conclusion: a loss at the same membership, and goals 3 and 4 hold together only with a very large increase in members. Both responses use reading (b) and label it, so their figures count as correct by a different route.
- No price cut, 25 % margin: 150n − 30n − 11,900 = 37.5n → 82.5n = 11,900 → n = 144.2 → **145 (+25)**.
- At €120, revenue returns to €18,000 at exactly **150 members**.
- Each extra €1,000/month of fixed cost: to cover it, 1,000/120 ≈ 9 members at €150 and 1,000/90 ≈ 12 at €120. To keep a 25 % margin, 1,000/82.5 ≈ 12 at €150 and 1,000/60 ≈ 17 at €120.
- Tax arrears €9,000 / 2,500 = **3.6 months of all profit**; spread over 12 months = €750/month = 30 % of profit. It is an obligation, not a goal.
- Forecast at €120 (reading b): 150 members → 18,000 − 11,900 − 4,500 = €1,600 (8.9 %); 180 → 21,600 − 17,300 = €4,300 (19.9 %); 199 → 23,880 − 17,870 = €6,010 (25.2 %).
- Losing members at today's price: 10 lost → €1,300; 21 lost → ≈ 0; profit under €1,000 ↔ ≈ 13 members lost (1,500 / 120 = 12.5).
- Reserve/cash: **not given**.

## Step 3. Forbidden items and invented facts

It does not invent the reserve or the competitor's price; it asks for both. It stays within 700 words. This is framed conditionally and does not change the advice. "Three to five [goals] are usually enough" is advice, not a stated fact.
