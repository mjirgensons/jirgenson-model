## Step 1 — Recomputed key numbers

- Revenue €18,000 from 120 members, so €150 per member. Costs €15,500, of which €3,600 variable and €11,900 fixed. Profit €2,500 a month. Margin 2,500 / 18,000 = **13.9 %**.
- Variable cost is €3,600 / 120 = €30 per member. That is the same as 20 % of revenue at today's price. Contribution per member at €150 = €120. Break-even at €150 = 11,900 / 120 = 99.2, so **≈100 members**.
- **The two readings of a −20 % price cut (€120):**
  - Gold reading (variable cost = 20 % of revenue): 14,400 − 11,900 − 2,880 = **−€380 a month**. A 25 % margin needs revenue 11,900 / 0.55 ≈ €21,636, so **≈180 members (+50 %)**.
  - Literal reading (the task says the €3,600 "vary with the number of members", so it stays €30 per member): 14,400 − 11,900 − 3,600 = **−€1,100 a month**. Contribution per member is €90. Break-even = 11,900 / 90 = 132.2, so **133 members**. A 25 % margin needs (90 − 30)·n = 11,900, so n = 198.3, about **199 members (+66 %)**.
  - Both readings lead to the same advice: the cut makes a loss at today's member count and needs +50–66 % more members to reach 25 %. Both responses use the literal per-member reading. That is a valid alternative route.
- **25 % margin at €150:** (120 − 37.5)·n = 11,900, so n = 144.2, about **145 members (+25, +21 %)**.
- **Revenue back to €18,000 at €120:** 150 members (+30).
- **Each extra €1,000 of fixed cost:**
  - To keep profit unchanged: 1,000 / 120 ≈ 8 members at €150, or 1,000 / 90 ≈ 11 members at €120.
  - To keep a 25 % margin: 1,000 / 82.5 ≈ 12 members at €150, or 1,000 / 60 ≈ 17 members at €120.
- **Tax arrears:** €9,000 = 3.6 months of current profit. Spread over 12 months it is €750 a month, which is 30 % of current profit. It is an obligation (penalties, enforcement), not an optional goal.
- **Reserve / cash:** not given.
