- Cash: 22,000 → 13,000 = −9,000 over 6 months = **−1,500/month**.

- Months to zero at −1,500/month: 13,000 / 1,500 ≈ **8.7 months**.

  - Nothing changes, pace −1,500 for ~3 months: 13,000 − 4,500 = 8,500 by mid-January ✓.
  - If receivables stop growing, cash flow = 3,000 − 2,333 ≈ +667/month: 13,000 + 2,000 = 15,000 ✓.

  - Plan works, half of 13,000 back: 15,000 + 6,500 ≈ 21,500 (stated ≈ 21,000) ✓. All of it back: 15,000 + 13,000 = 28,000 ✓.
