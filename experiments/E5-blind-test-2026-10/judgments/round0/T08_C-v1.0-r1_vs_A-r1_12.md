## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Cash: €22,000 → €13,000 = −€9,000 over 6 months = **−€1,500/month**.
- Receivables: €8,000 → €21,000 = **+€13,000** (≈ +€2,167/month).

- Months to zero at −€1,500/month: 13,000 / 1,500 ≈ **8.7 months**.

I also checked Response 1's forecasts against one consistent model: cash change = +€667/month underlying, minus receivable growth.

- Worse case: €10,000 more sinks by end January. 13,000 + 2,333 − 10,000 ≈ €5,333. This path reaches €8,000 around late December.
