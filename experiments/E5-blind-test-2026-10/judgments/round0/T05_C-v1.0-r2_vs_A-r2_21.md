## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Profit: 7,200 − 6,900 = €300/month, which is 300 / 7,200 = 4.2 %.

- No-shows only to 15 % (+€450), no price rise: 750/month, so 4,400 / 750 ≈ 5.9 months (≈ 6).
- With half of slots refilled: halving is worth +€450. Plus the price rise: 300 + 450 + 360 = 1,110, so 4,400 / 1,110 ≈ 4.0 months.
