## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Average income: (6,100 + 6,900 + 6,500) / 3 = 19,500 / 3 = **€6,500**.
- Biggest client: 0.55 × 6,500 = **€3,575**. Second client: 0.15 × 6,500 = **€975**. All others: 0.30 × 6,500 = **€1,950**.
- After the second client leaves: 6,500 − 975 = **€5,525**.
- Biggest client's share after the loss: 3,575 / 5,525 = 0.647, so **≈ 65 %**.
- Gap to €10,000: 10,000 − 5,525 = **€4,475**, and 10,000 / 5,525 = 1.81, so **+81 %** in 12 months. €4,475 / 12 ≈ €373 a month of added monthly income. At €450 a month the target is reached in 4,475 / 450 ≈ 9.9, so about 10 months.
- If the biggest client stayed at €3,575, they would be 35.75 %, so ≈ 36 % of a €10,000 business. If the biggest client also left: €1,950 remains.
- Not given in the task: reserve, monthly costs, contract terms, pipeline.

Response 1's arithmetic matches all of these: 5,525; 65 %; 3,575 / 975 / 1,950; 4,475; ≈ 370 per month; 450 per month reaching the target in 10 months; 36 %; 1,950. Its sensitivity range of roughly €5,300–5,700, for shares a few points off, is reasonable (for example, a 12–18 % second client gives 5,330–5,720). Response 2's numbers are rounded but correct: ≈ 5,500; ≈ 4,500 gap; ≈ 80 %; ≈ 3,600; "about 30 %" for the rest. Response 2 never states the post-loss concentration of about 65 %.

Word counts, counting headings and excluding list numerals: Response 1 ≈ 712 words. Response 2 ≈ 347 words.

Question marks addressed to the person: Response 1 has 5, one per numbered question, and no other question marks. Response 2 has 16, spread across 8 numbered questions.

## Step 4. Criteria

### Response 1
- **C1 = 7.** All arithmetic is correct, and the two key assumptions are labelled (the work stops completely; income means invoiced revenue before costs), with a sensitivity range. However, the market-wide AI shift and the "firmer ground" niches are asserted as fact without labels.
- **C2 = 8.** It finds the central issue: concentration rising to 65 % against a stated red line. It names the nearest danger (cash when the second client's work stops, with the reserve unknown) and the biggest danger (losing the main client leaves €1,950). The link between AI and the main client is only asked, not reasoned through.
- **C3 = 9.** Two concrete steps the owner can take, both due by Monday, fast and reversible. There is an explicit leave-alone list and a first check on 31 October.
- **C4 = 4.** The language is everyday and the response stops after 5 questions. But it runs about 42 % over the 500-word budget and delivers a near-full brief in a round that asked for a preliminary reading and questions. That is two clear breaches of the round format.
- **C5 = 9.** The goal order is proposed with the choice left to the owner. The red line (45 %) comes with a reason and is tested against the forecast ("the forecast moves you further in"). A 12-month target cap (30 %), two monthly metrics, a review date and a leave-alone list complete it.
- **C6 = 8.** No promises. It gives a range under stated conditions and a nothing-changes forecast, and defers the plan-works forecast "as a range, not a promise". The confident assertion about the market is a slight overreach.

### Response 2
- **C1 = 9.** Numbers are correct within rounding, there are no invented facts, and the forecast is stated conditionally ("if nothing replaces it").
- **C2 = 5.** It sees the main-client exposure only partly. It does not quantify the post-loss concentration, does not name the nearest danger or its timing, and does not ask about reserves. Good diagnostic questions (revenue vs take-home, why the client switched) partly compensate.
- **C3 = 2.** There is no action of any kind, although the round called for one safe action now.
- **C4 = 5.** It is within the word budget, plain-spoken, and delivers no brief. But it asks 8 questions (16 question marks) against a limit of 5 and does not rank them by effect.
- **C5 = 3.** Only a measurable cap (25–30 %) is proposed, with the choice left to the owner. There is no priority order, no red line tested against the forecast, no measurement frequency, no review date and no leave-alone list.
- **C6 = 6.** It is honest and conditional, with no promises, but thin: no explicit forecast and no named risky assumption.
