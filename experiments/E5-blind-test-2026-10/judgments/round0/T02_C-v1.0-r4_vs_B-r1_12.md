# Judgment — T02 (Food truck, 72-hour permit clock, EMERGENCY, EN)

## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Opening cash: €1,900. Deposit today €1,000 → €900 left (day 0).
- Repair balance: €2,400 − €1,000 = €1,400, assumed due on repair day (day 2) → €900 − €1,400 = **−€500** on day 2.
- Wages €1,200 on day 4 → −€500 − €1,200 = **−€1,700** (lowest point, before any inflow).
- Wedding deposit +€1,500 on day 5 → −€1,700 + €1,500 = **−€200**.
- Totals check: out €1,000 + €1,400 + €1,200 = €3,600; available €1,900 + €1,500 = €3,400 → −€200.
- If the wedding client pays the full €1,500 before day 2: day 2 = €900 + €1,500 − €1,400 = +€1,000; day 4 = +€1,000 − €1,200 = −€200.
- "Nothing changes" path (no deposit, no repair): €1,900 → €700 after wages (day 4) → €2,200 on day 5, but the permit is suspended at about day 2 and the €600 of stock is at risk.
- Clock: inspection was yesterday, so 72 h ends on day 2 (day after tomorrow) at about the hour of the inspection. The repair slot is also day 2, so there is no slack. Nearest irreversible damage = permit suspension. Food held at 9°C (above the 4°C limit) may be unsafe, and about €600 of perishable stock is at risk.
