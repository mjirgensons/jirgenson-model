# Judgment — T04 (Translation agency, round 1, FULL, EN)

## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Three-month average: (6,100 + 6,900 + 6,500) / 3 = 19,500 / 3 = **€6,500**.
- Biggest client: 0.55 × 6,500 = **€3,575**. Second client: 0.15 × 6,500 = **€975**. Everyone else: 0.30 × 6,500 = **€1,950**.
- After the second client leaves: 6,500 − 975 = **€5,525**.
- Biggest client's new share: 3,575 / 5,525 = 0.647, so **≈ 65 %**.
- Gap to €10,000: 10,000 − 5,525 = **€4,475**, which is 4,475 / 5,525 = **+81 %** in 12 months ("about 80 %" is acceptable).
- Other checks used: if the biggest client also went, 5,525 − 3,575 = €1,950. A 30 % cut at the biggest client: 5,525 − 1,072.5 ≈ €4,450. Annual loss from the second client: 975 × 12 = €11,700. Biggest client at €10,000 with unchanged volume: 3,575 / 10,000 ≈ 36 %. One-third threshold at €10,000 ≈ €3,333.
- Not given: reserve, monthly costs, contract terms, pipeline.
- Budget: preliminary reading plus ≤5 questions, no full brief, ≤500 words.
