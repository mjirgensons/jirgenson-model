# Judgment: T08, Response 1 vs Response 2

## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Cash: 22,000 → 13,000 = −9,000 over 6 months = **−1,500/month**.
- Receivables: 8,000 → 21,000 = **+13,000** (≈ +2,167/month).
- Paper profit: 3,000 × 6 = 18,000. Expected cash change +18,000 against actual −9,000 gives a **27,000 gap**. Receivables explain 13,000 (48%, "just under half"), so **≈14,000 is unexplained** (≈2,333/month). Possible causes: drawings, loan principal, equipment, VAT or tax, other working-capital movements, or a wrong profit figure. This is a question for the owner, not a fact.
- Reserve: 13,000 / 26,000 = **0.5 months** (about two weeks). That is red against a proposed 1-month line, and the balance was already under that line six months ago (22,000 < 26,000).
- Months to zero at −1,500/month: 13,000 / 1,500 ≈ **8.7 months**. At the worse pace of −2,000/month it is 6.5 months.
- Big customer: 40% × 21,000 ≈ **8,400** of the receivables, if their share of receivables matches their share of sales.
- With a labelled assumption (sales = 26,000 + 3,000 = 29,000/month), the big customer is ≈11,600/month. Moving from 30 to 90 days ties up 2 × 11,600 ≈ **23,200**. With 13,000 already built up, **up to ≈10,000 more** could still build up.
- If the big customer pays nothing for one month: outflow 26,000 minus inflow from others (60% × 29,000 = 17,400) = −8,600, which leaves ≈4,400.
- The cash generated if the 14,000 recurs: 3,000 − 2,333 ≈ **+667/month** even with on-time payment.
- Trigger check: from 13,000 at −1,500/month, the balance crosses 10,000 in 2 months (mid-December from mid-October). At −2,000/month it crosses after 1.5 months (end of November). At −1,500/month the balance is ≈7,000 after 4 months (mid-February).

It shows the alarm was already ringing six months ago. It does not state the zero horizon and does not question the profit figure itself.
- **C3 = 8.** Every action has an owner and a time ("Today or tomorrow, you", "By Friday, you or your accountant", "This week, you", "From now on"), plus an escalation rule. It lacks the by-customer invoice list and a part-payment ask.
