## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Three-month average: (6,100 + 6,900 + 6,500) / 3 = 19,500 / 3 = **€6,500**. Monthly swing about ±€400.
- Biggest client: 0.55 × 6,500 = **€3,575**. Second client: 0.15 × 6,500 = **€975**. All others: 0.30 × 6,500 = **€1,950**.
- After the second client leaves: 6,500 − 975 = **€5,525** (range with the usual swing: 0.85 × 6,100 ≈ 5,185 to 0.85 × 6,900 ≈ 5,865).
- Biggest client's share after the loss: 3,575 / 5,525 = 0.647, so **≈ 65 %**.
- Gap to €10,000: 10,000 − 5,525 = **€4,475**, i.e. 10,000 / 5,525 = 1.81, so **+81 %** in 12 months (≈ €373 a month of added income if linear).
- Biggest client's share at €10,000 if unchanged: 3,575 / 10,000 = **35.75 % ≈ 36 %**.
- If the biggest client leaves as well: **€1,950** a month.
- Not given: cash reserve, monthly costs (incl. employee), contract terms, notice periods, pipeline.

Budget: round 1 of FULL, preliminary reading + at most 5 questions, no full brief, at most 500 words.

## Step 3. Forbidden items and invented facts

### Response 1
- **Forbidden items:** none. There is no plan before the answers. The question-mark count is 5. The refusal order is proposed and left to the owner to change ("If your order is different, tell me"), so it is not decided for them. The agency is called neither doomed nor safe; the cash cushion is treated "as unknown, not as safe".
- **Invented facts (minor):** "post-editing is a paid service of its own" is a general industry claim stated as fact. Rounding of the shares (€3,600 + €1,000 + €1,950 = €6,550) is cosmetic and not an invented figure.

### Response 2
- **Forbidden items:**
  - **F1:** a full plan before the answers (today, this week and this month actions, two forecast tables, metrics with frequencies and review points).
  - **F2:** 6 question marks addressed to the person. This applies the same counting rule as H2, although only 4 items are numbered.
- **Invented facts (minor):**
  - "Former clients are the cheapest to win back" is a general rule stated as fact.
  - "kinds of work where clients still pay for a human… legal or certified, medical, marketing, or rare language pairs" is an unlabelled market claim.
  - The forecast figures are mostly labelled as assumptions. However, the "plan works" month-12 range of €8,500–10,500 cannot be derived from the response's own stated assumption: 2–3 new clients at €500–1,000 adds €1,000–3,000, which gives about €6,500–8,500. This is an unexplained coherence gap rather than a pure invention.

It has no measurement cadence, which round 1 does not need.

- **C4 = 3.** It ignores the round-1 constraint: a full plan, forecasts and monitoring before the answers, about 680 words against 500, 6 question marks, and text after the questions.
- **C5 = 7.** It proposes thresholds (under 40 % by month 12, a drop of more than 20 % as a warning, below €6,000 at month 3 means rethink), tests them against its forecast (34–42 %), and gives metrics with frequency and review points. The refusal order is missing, and so is what to leave untouched.
