# Judgment: T09, Response 1 vs Response 2

## Step 1. Recomputed key numbers

- Turnover trend: 48,000 → 42,000 = −6,000 / 48,000 = **−12.5 %**; 42,000 → 37,000 = −5,000 / 42,000 = **−11.9 %**.
- 2026 run-rate: 25,000 / 9 = 2,778 per month (2025: 37,000 / 12 = 3,083 per month); × 12 = **≈ 33,300**, which is −9.9 % against 37,000 (**≈ −10 %**).
- +30 % on 33,300 = **≈ 43,300**. That is more than 2024 (42,000), in a town that has lost 25 % of its people (8,000 → 6,000) and its largest factory.
- Extra turnover needed: ≈ 10,000 a year, which is ≈ 30 € per working day at 6–7 days a week.
- Hours: 55 × 52 = 2,860 h, or 55 × 48 ≈ 2,640 h. 6,000 / 2,860 = **€2.10/h**; 6,000 / 2,640 = €2.27/h. So ≈ **€2–2.3 per hour**.
- Implied costs in 2025: 37,000 − 6,000 = 31,000, about 2,583 per month. The €4,000 reserve covers ≈ 1.5 months of costs.
- Nothing changes: another ≈ −10 % gives **≈ 30,000 in 2027**. Profit depends on how much of the cost is fixed. If costs stay at 31,000, profit is about −1,000 to 0. If costs fall with turnover, profit is about 4,800–5,000.
- The lease ends in 8 months, around June 2027. That is the natural decision point. A wrong exit costs little: €4,000 reserve, no stated long-term debts.
- Calendar check: 2026-10-16 is a Friday, so 19 October 2026 is a Monday.
