  - Today's profit: 14,400 / 90 = 160 members.
  - Today's revenue: 18,000 / 120 = 150 members.
  - 25 % margin: 90n − 11,900 ≥ 30n gives n ≥ 198.3, so **199 members (+66 %, +79)**.
  - Without the cut: 120n − 11,900 ≥ 37.5n gives n ≥ 144.2, so 145 members. Equivalently, €2,000/month less cost at 120 members (4,500 − 2,500).
  - Each extra €1,000/month of fixed cost:

  - The cut costs 120 × €30 = €3,600/month. That equals the contribution of 30 members at €120, i.e. 25 % of the members.
  - Sensitivity: with ±10 % on the variable split, break-even moves from 132.2 to 131.8 or 132.6, which is less than one member.

Both responses use the per-member route and are judged against those numbers. Both reach the gold conclusions by that route: the cut gives a loss at the same member count, and goals 3 and 4 need a large member increase (about +66 % on this route, +50 % on the gold route).
