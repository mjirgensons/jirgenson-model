## The tax arrears come first

The €9,000 is a bill, not a goal, and "sometime this year" is not a date. It equals 3.6 months of your entire current profit, so every goal that lowers profit pushes it back. Arrears usually keep collecting interest or penalties, and in most countries the tax office agrees an instalment plan more readily before it starts enforcement. Check your country's rules.

First step this week: ask the tax office for a written instalment plan. As an illustration, €750 a month over 12 months would take 30% of today's profit.

## Where the plan breaks

**1. The 20% price cut cancels three other goals.** At €120 per member, your 120 members bring €14,400 against costs of €15,500. That is a loss of about €1,100 a month. You would need 133 members just to break even, and 150 just to get back to today's €18,000 of revenue. The 25% margin would need about 199 members, roughly two-thirds more than now.

I am assuming the cut applies to every member. If only new members got it, the damage would be smaller, but existing members would usually ask for the same price. Prices are also usually hard to raise again once lowered.

You have not said how many members left since the new studio opened, so you may be giving up €3,600 of monthly revenue to answer a threat of unknown size. How long you could carry a loss depends on your cash, which you have not mentioned either.

**2. The 25% margin by December does not fit the spending plans.** It means €4,500 profit a month, €2,000 more than now. At today's price that needs about 145 members (+25), and only if costs stay as they are. Two teachers, a second room and an app all add monthly cost, and none is priced. As a yardstick, every €1,000 of new fixed cost needs about 9 extra members at €150 (about 12 at €120) just to pay for itself.

**3. Eleven goals is too many, and several are not goals.** Eleven split your attention; three to five are usually enough for a year. The price cut, the app, the teachers and the second room are ways of doing something. They serve one goal: keeping and winning members against the new studio. "Higher revenue" repeats "more members". "More members", "work less" and "better reviews" have no number or date, so in December you could not say whether they worked.

**4. "Work less myself" runs against "open a second room".** A new room usually takes more of the owner's hours at first (hiring, scheduling, teething problems) before it frees any. You have not said how many hours you work now, so there is nothing to measure progress against.

## The smaller points

- Fewer than 3 complaints a month is measurable. Decide who counts them and what counts as one. New teachers and a new room usually raise complaints at first.
- Better reviews needs a current rating, a target and a date.
- The app has no cost and no stated purpose. Tie it to keeping members, or let it wait.
