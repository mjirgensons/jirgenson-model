**Straight answer**

Nobody can give you an exact monthly profit or a guaranteed payback: it depends on how fast the new diary fills. The fit-out (€28,000) is almost twice your reserve (€15,000).

**The numbers**

Salon 1 leaves €2,200 a month after your €2,000 pay. Salon 2 costs €5,000 a month before the first client: rent €1,800 plus two technicians at €3,200. I assume €1,600 is your full cost per person (if net pay, employer contributions usually come on top; check yours) and materials and running costs take about 15% of revenue (illustration). Break-even is then about €5,900 revenue a month (€5,560 at 10%, €6,670 at 25%).

Steady monthly result: €6,000 revenue gives about €0, €8,000 gives about €1,800, €11,000 (as good as salon 1) gives about €4,350.

Payback with start-up losses: I assume revenue averages €3,000 in months 1-3 and €6,000 in months 4-6 (illustration). Those six months lose about €7,050, so you must recover about €35,000. If revenue is €11,000 a month from month 7, payback is about 14 months from opening; at €8,000, about 26; at €6,000, never.

**Danger and plan**

The danger is the day you sign the lease and pay the fit-out. From then €5,000 a month is fixed whatever the clients do, and the first months lose about €2,450 a month (my assumption), more than the €2,200 salon 1 can spare. Paying from the reserve crosses my proposed red line (one month of expenses, €8,800) on day one. Outside money needed: about €29,000 if you keep the €8,800 cushion.

My proposed order, last to first given up: salon 1 and your pay, the €8,800 cushion, salon 2. So do not sign, buy or hire before funding is in writing.

- Get a written funding offer (interest lengthens the payback above).
- Open with one technician: fixed costs fall to €3,400, break-even to about €4,000. Hire the second when the first is booked about 80% for four weeks (usual rule of thumb).

Weekly: bookings for the next two weeks (share of technician hours) and cash. Month 3 revenue below €4,000: stop and review. Next review: 30 October, with a funding offer in hand.

Forecasts: opened as planned with the diary stuck at €6,000 or less, no payback; at €8,000-11,000, 26 to 14 months. The range narrows most with revenue per technician in salon 1, weeks salon 2 needs to fill, and a technician's real cost.
