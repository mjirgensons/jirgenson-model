**What the numbers show**

No honest analysis can give an exact monthly figure or guarantee a payback period, because what the second salon takes in does not exist yet. The second salon already costs €5,000 a month before the first client (rent €1,800, technicians €3,200). If supplies, utilities and card fees take about 15% of takings (my illustration), it needs about €5,900 a month to break even. Payback counts the €28,000 fit-out plus about €4,000 lost while the diary fills over three months (illustration: takings €2,500, €4,500, €6,000).

| Steady takings | Result per month | Payback from opening |
|---|---|---|
| €6,000 | about €100 | practically never |
| €8,000 | about €1,800 | about 21 months |
| €11,000 (like salon 1) | about €4,350 | about 10 months |

A new salon usually takes months to fill its diary, so the last row is the best end, not the plan; the first is the worst end.

**The greatest danger**

A slow start hitting a thin reserve. After opening your monthly fixed costs are €13,800 (€8,800 plus €5,000), so your €15,000 is 1.1 months. I propose a red line of one month, €13,800. Your first salon leaves €2,200 after your pay; I assume it stays in the account. If takings stay at €2,500 for three months, the reserve crosses the line in month 2: that is an alarm already ringing, not a margin. I assume the €28,000 comes from a loan or other money, since your €15,000 cannot cover it; repayments would lengthen every payback above.

**What to do**

- Hire one technician first, the second when the first one's diary is mostly full. Fixed costs fall to €3,400 and break-even takings to about €4,000; that is the plan-works case.
- Keep €10,000 to €15,000 aside for the second salon's first two to three months, on top of your reserve. Do not sign or order equipment before the €28,000 and this cushion are secured.
- Protect, last to give up: the first salon and your €2,000 pay, then the reserve, then the second salon's schedule (my proposed order; tell me if yours differs).
- Check weekly: booked share of each technician's hours, takings, reserve. Review after months 1 and 3. If month-3 takings are under about €5,000 or the reserve is under €13,800, stop adding costs.

The range narrows most with bookings you can count before opening, how the €28,000 is funded, and your real running costs.
