**Where you stand**

A season month earns €3,300 after parts (30%) and all fixed costs; a winter month loses €3,700. Over a year that is +€23,100 and −€18,500, about +€4,600 after your own €1,500 pay.

I am assuming October brings the season average, so you start November with about €12,300. October is usually weaker: each €1,000 less revenue costs €700 of cash. Then €4,900 at the end of December, €1,200 at the end of January, zero around 10 February, −€6,200 at the end of March. A 10% error in winter sales shifts the gap by about €1,400, so the conclusion holds.

**The greatest danger**

Around 10 February the account is empty while rent and your mechanic's €3,500 are due; without him the season opens with no repair capacity. If you bought the bikes at the end of October, you would have about €300 on 1 November, the account would be empty in the first week, and the March gap would be €18,200.

My proposed red line is €3,700 in the account, one winter month's loss; below it you cannot cover the next month's bills. Today's €9,000 is above it, but your numbers cross it around 10 January, so the fix belongs in October and November.
