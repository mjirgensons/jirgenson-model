**Where you stand**

My guess at your order: the concert is the last thing you'd give up, because its date can't move, and the scooter's June 1 date is the first to slide. If yours is different, tell me. Keeping June 1 for the scooter would mean waiting on the ticket.

**What to do**

- This week, ask your neighbour for two more babysitting evenings a month. If €40 is about four evenings (my assumption, roughly €10 each), that is +€20 a month.
- Find one more small job: another neighbour, dog walking, washing cars, or selling things you don't use. That is the next +€20.
- Put the €25 pocket money into savings the day it arrives, somewhere you can't easily spend it.
