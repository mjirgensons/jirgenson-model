## What I would do before 21 October

- Ask the second client for their exact last day, and whether they would still pay for a human check of the AI output or for the texts the tool handles badly. Firms that adopt a tool often keep a reviewer.

- Do not cut anyone's hours, take on new fixed costs or lower your prices to win volume until I have your cost figures.
