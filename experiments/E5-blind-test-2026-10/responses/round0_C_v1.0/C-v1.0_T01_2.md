Hold off on the e-bikes. The shop earns money over a year, but winter takes your account to zero around 10 February. This week, line up a standby overdraft and set your winter pay.

**Where you stand**

A season month leaves €3,300 (€14,000 revenue, €4,200 parts, €6,500 fixed costs). A winter month loses €3,700 (€4,000, €1,200, €6,500). Over the year the shop earns about €4,600 after your own pay; the e-bikes cost 2.6 times that.

If nothing changes, cash is about €12,300 at the end of October, €8,600 in November, €4,900 in December, €1,200 in January, below zero around 10 February, and about −€6,200 at the end of March. Paid from the account, the bikes would empty it in early November.

**The biggest danger**

The February rent and wages. With no cash you cannot pay the mechanic's €3,500, and a mechanic who leaves in February is not there in April. I propose a red line of €3,700 in the account, one more winter month's loss. You cross it around 10 January, which is early enough to act.

**What to do**

This week, by you:
- Do not sign or pay for the bikes.
- Ask the bank, while the account is healthy, for a standby overdraft of about €8,000 (the €6,200 gap plus €1,800 cushion).
- Set your winter pay. Each €500 less a month, November to March, is worth €2,500. My forecast assumes you pay yourself €500 a month in winter; the figure is yours.

This month:
- Raise winter takings: each extra €1,000 of takings a month leaves you €700 more, €3,500 by March.
- Ask the landlord and the mechanic about lower winter rent or hours, agreed in writing; each €500 a month is worth €2,500.

**What I would protect first**

My order, from what you would give up last to first (tell me if yours differs): the shop with rent and the mechanic paid on time; then your own pay; then the e-bikes, which wait.

**What not to do yet**

Do not buy or lease the bikes until March cash stays above €3,700 after the payments and you can count real bookings (pre-sold vouchers will tell you). Each €500 a month of payments makes the winter loss €4,200. Do not cut the mechanic's hours or pay without agreement.
