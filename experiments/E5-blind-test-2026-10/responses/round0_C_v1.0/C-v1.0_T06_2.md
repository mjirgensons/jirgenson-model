**The numbers**

I can't promise an exact profit or payback; they depend on what the second salon takes in each month, which I don't have.

The second salon costs €5,000 a month before supplies (rent €1,800, two technicians €3,200). I assume supplies, utilities and advertising add 15% of takings, so break-even is about €5,900 a month (€5,600 at 10%, €6,300 at 20%). If the €1,600 is before employer taxes, add about €650–800 a month.

I also assume a slow start (my illustration): €3,000 of takings a month for three months, then €5,500 for three. That loses about €8,300, so you must earn back €36,300, not €28,000.

| Takings once established | Profit per month | Payback from opening |
|---|---|---|
| €5,500 (half the first salon) | about −€300 | never |
| €8,250 (three quarters) | about +€2,000 | about 24 months |
| €11,000 (same as the first) | about +€4,350 | about 15 months |

**What can hurt you**

The danger is the day you pay for the fit-out. The reserve is €15,000, 1.7 months of the first salon's costs; the fit-out is €28,000. My proposed red line is €14,000 (one month's costs for both salons); paying from savings crosses it at once. Finance it outside the reserve and add the repayments to the costs above.

The first salon carries the slow start: after your pay it leaves €2,200 a month, €13,200 over six months, against the €8,300 loss. If its takings fall by a fifth, that surplus is zero and the reserve pays. I would protect, in this order (my proposal; tell me if yours differs): the first salon and your pay, then the reserve, then the second salon.

Not yet: signing before the money is arranged, or hiring both technicians before the first is fully booked.

**Forecasts and checks**

As it stands, the fit-out takes the whole reserve and leaves €13,000 missing. If it is financed outside the reserve and takings reach €8,250 from month 7: about +€2,000 a month, payback about 24 months (best 15, worst never).

On the 5th of each month, check takings of the second salon (€5,900), the first (€11,000) and the reserve (€14,000). Below €5,500 at month 6, pause new costs. Your first salon's bookings and prices would narrow the range. Next check: the day before you sign the fit-out contract.
