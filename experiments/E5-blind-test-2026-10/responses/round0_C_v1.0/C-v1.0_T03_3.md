**The 20% price cut is the biggest problem**

At €120 a member, your 120 members bring in €14,400 a month against costs of €15,500. Today's profit of €2,500 a month (14% of revenue) becomes a loss of €1,100. Each member now leaves €120 after the €30 they cost you; at the new price that falls to €90, a quarter less, while the €11,900 of fixed costs stays. So the cut demands:

- 133 members just to break even (13 more than now)
- 150 members to earn today's revenue again (+30)
- 160 members to earn today's profit again (+40)
- 199 members for your 25% margin (+79)

At today's price, 25% needs 145 members (+25). The cut means about three times as many new members for the same margin, and it is hard to undo, because raising prices later costs you members. If your €3,600 for member-related costs is 10% off either way, break-even stays at 132 to 133 members, so this holds.
