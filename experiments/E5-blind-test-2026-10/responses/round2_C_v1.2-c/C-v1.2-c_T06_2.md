I cannot give you an exact monthly figure or a guaranteed payback period. Nobody can, because the one number that decides both, what the second salon earns each month, does not exist yet. What I can give you is what your numbers already fix, and a range for the rest.

## Where you are

- The first salon leaves you €2,200 a month after your own €2,000 pay (€11,000 minus €8,800).
- Your reserve of €15,000 is 1.7 months of the first salon's expenses.
- The second salon costs at least €5,000 a month before any supplies (rent €1,800 plus two technicians at €3,200). It must bring in more than that just to stand still.
- The fit-out is €28,000. That is €13,000 more than your whole reserve.

## The greatest danger

The danger is the day you sign the lease and pay for the fit-out. I propose a red line of one month of combined expenses, about €13,800 (€8,800 plus €5,000). Your reserve is already close to that line (1.09 months), and paying for the fit-out takes it to zero. This is not a line you might cross later. It is crossed on that day, and one slow month in either salon would then hit the first salon, which is what pays you.

## What the second salon could make

I am assuming supplies and other running costs of 10% of its revenue. This is an illustration, so replace it with your real figure. I also take the second salon's revenue as a share of the first salon's:

| Second salon reaches | Revenue | Monthly result |
|---|---|---|
| 30% of the first | €3,300 | about -€2,030 |
| 50% of the first | €5,500 | about -€50 |
| 80% of the first | €8,800 | about +€2,920 |

Payback includes the start-up months. If it stays at 30% to 50% for the first six months, the loss is about €0 to €12,000. After that, only the 80% case pays the €28,000 back: about €40,000 to recover at €2,920 a month, so roughly 20 months from opening. At 50% or less it does not pay back at all. The range narrows most with three items: how many bookings you can really fill in the first six months, your real running costs, and how the €13,000 gap is financed.

## What not to do

Do not sign the lease or pay the fit-out from the reserve alone. Do not hire both technicians before the first bookings are there.
