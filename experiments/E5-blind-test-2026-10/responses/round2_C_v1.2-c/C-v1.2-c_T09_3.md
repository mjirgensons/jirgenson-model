+30% would mean about €43,000 next year. Your numbers show about €33,000 this year (€31,000–35,000; I assume the last three months are like the average ones) and a decline of 10–12% every year, while the town has a quarter fewer residents. This suggests that the main force is the town, not your kiosk (I cannot prove that). Marketing can help stop the decline; I do not promise 30% growth. So I suggest a cheap 8-week test, not a big plan.

## Where you are
Last year's profit of €6,000 for 55 hours a week (about 2,860 hours a year) is about €2 an hour and 16% of turnover. This year, with the same share, it will come to about €5,300. Even if +30% worked, the extra profit would be about €1,600 (if the new sales have the same share). Your time is as big a problem as turnover.

## The biggest danger
The end of the lease in 8 months (around June). Check in the lease by what date you must give notice about extending, because it may be earlier. If you sign for a long time and the decline continues, turnover in 2029 will be about €24,000, but you will pay rent for the whole term. If you leave too early, you will lose about €5,300 of income a year, and the transition has to be funded from the €4,000 savings.

My suggested red line: turnover below €2,100 a month (€25,000 a year) for three months in a row; that gives about €4,000 of profit, about €1.4 an hour. Right now it is €2,780 a month; on the trend, the line would be crossed in 2028–2029, that is, during a long lease. I cannot assess whether the savings are enough while I do not know the monthly expenses.

## What to do
This week:
- Pull sales from the till by day, hour and product group (or count by hand for two weeks). If there are no customers in some hours, shorten the opening hours: it gives you back your time at no cost and is reversible.
- Ask the property owner whether a 12-month lease with an exit option is possible.
- From 19 October to 14 December, try one offer, for example home delivery of the press: no more than €300 and 3 hours a week. Before advertising lotteries, check with the lottery operator what may be advertised.

## What to protect and what not to do
My assumption about the order (if yours is different, tell me, and I will recalculate): the longest, the €4,000 savings, then your time and health, then the kiosk profit above ~€4,000; we give up +30% first. Do not spend more than €300 of the savings, do not sign a long lease before the 14 December data, and do not buy extra stock "for growth".

## What to measure
Every Monday: last week's turnover against the same week last year (without action about −10%; goal 0% or better). Next check on 14 December. If it is still −10% or worse then, we drop +30% and plan a shorter lease or fewer hours; if 0% or better, we continue.

If the test works (the decline stopped): about €33,000–35,000, profit about €5,300–5,600. €43,000 is outside this range.
