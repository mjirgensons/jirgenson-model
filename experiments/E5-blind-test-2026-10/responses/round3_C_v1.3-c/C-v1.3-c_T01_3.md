**Where you stand**

A season month leaves €14,000 − €4,200 parts − €6,500 fixed = +€3,300. A winter month leaves €4,000 − €1,200 − €6,500 = −€3,700. Over the year that is +€4,600 after your own pay: the e-bikes cost 2.6 years of it.

**The biggest danger**

Winter hitting a thin reserve. Assuming October is an average month, you enter November with about €12,300 and lose €3,700 a month. The account is empty around 10 February with rent and the mechanic still due, and March ends €6,200 short. If October is weak (say €10,000 of sales, an illustration), it empties in mid-January and March ends €9,000 short: the worst end.

My proposed red line: €3,700 in the account, one winter month of losses; below it, paying rent and wages is no longer safe. The no-change forecast crosses it around 10 January.

My proposed order, yours to change: last to give up, the shop getting through winter with rent and the mechanic paid; then your own pay; first to go, the e-bikes. Until the account is back above the red line: no new purchases, no rushed loan for the bikes.
