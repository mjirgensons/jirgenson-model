30% means roughly +€10,000 (from ~33,300 to ~€43,300), while turnover has been falling for the third year already. I cannot justify that with marketing alone, and no honest calculation gives a guarantee. More realistic: stop the decline and do not let the lease renewal drive you into difficulties.

## Where you are

Turnover fell by 12.5% (2024) and 11.9% (2025). €25,000 in nine months is ~€33,300 a year, so another −10%; since 2023 −31% in total. The population has fallen by 25% in five years (~5.6% a year), so about half of the decline may simply be fewer people; the other half marketing can touch. Profit of €6,000 (16%) for ~2,800 hours (55 × 52) is about €2 an hour. The €4,000 savings are ~1.5 months of all expenses (I assume expenses of €31,000 a year, with the purchase of goods).

## The biggest danger

The lease renewal. The lease ends around 17 June 2027; the decision must be made by 15 March (I assume a 3-month notice period; check in the lease). The cost of a mistake:

- staying too long (in a multi-year lease with falling turnover): profit in 2027 about €4,900 or less (~€1.8 an hour), the rent eats the savings;
- leaving too early: you lose €400–500 a month with no replacement, only the €4,000 savings are left.

For the savings I suggest a red line of €2,600 (one month of expenses). Now the savings are above it; the forecast does not cross the line within the year, if you do not take more out of the kiosk than the profit.

## What to do

This week
- For four weeks write down the number of receipts and the amount by hour and by group (press, lottery, other goods).
- Check the lease renewal terms and whether the contracts with the lottery operator and the press distributor contain minimum turnover requirements.

This month
- Ask 20 regular customers what else they would buy from you; bring in one requested product as a test.
- Based on the data, shorten the opening hours in the weakest hours (can be undone at any time).
- If the town has a residents' group on social media, post an offer there (€0–50).

By 15 March: ask the landlord for 12 months with an exit option and lower rent, using the −31% as the argument.

For the tests, no more than €500 in total; at 16% profit they pay off if they create at least ~€3,100 of extra turnover.

## What to protect and what not to do

My suggestion (if your order is different, tell me): first the savings above €2,600, then the kiosk profit (€6,000 last year), then your 55 hours a week; the +30% goal is the first that can be dropped. Do not: sign a multi-year lease before March, or buy new goods at the expense of the savings without customer demand.

## Forecasts and control

If nothing changes: 2027 turnover about €30,000 (29,000–31,000), if the decline continues at 10–12% a year; profit about €4,900.

If the plan works: €31,500–34,000 (from −5% to +2% against 2026), profit €5,100–5,500. This is my estimate, not a promise; €43,300 is not in this range.

Every Monday compare the number of receipts and the amount with the same week last year; the first review on 16 November. If by 14 December the decline is still −10% or worse, stop the tests and do not buy anything new.
