## Where the plan breaks, most important first

**1. The 20% price cut works against your revenue, margin and arrears goals.** At €120 instead of €150, your 120 members bring €14,400, not €18,000. Against €15,500 of costs that is a loss of €1,100 a month from the start, with nothing left for the arrears. Each member then adds €90 after their own €30 of variable cost, so you need 160 members just to earn today's €2,500, and 199 for a 25% margin, with fixed costs unchanged. I am assuming the cut covers current members too (for new sign-ups only, the damage is much smaller) and that the €30 per member holds; a 10% error in the €3,600 moves the 160 by one or two members. The cut is also a method: the goal behind it is keeping members from leaving for the new studio, and you have not said how many are leaving.

**2. The 25% margin needs 145 members before any new cost.** Today's margin is 14%. With fixed costs of €11,900 and the €150 price, 25% needs 145 members, 25 more. Two teachers and a second room add fixed cost, but the plan gives neither amounts nor the members they bring, so I cannot say whether the margin goal fits with them.

**3. Eleven goals split your attention;** three to five are enough for a year. The price cut, app, two teachers and second room are methods: judge each by what it costs and which goal it serves. Goals 1 and 2 are one goal, since revenue is members times price.
