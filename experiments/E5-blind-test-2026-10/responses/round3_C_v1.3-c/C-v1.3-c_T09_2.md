+30% would mean about €43,000 next year, and your numbers do not support it. A realistic goal is to stop the decline and not make a mistake with the lease. I know a 55-hour week is hard.

## Where you are

Turnover is falling for the third year: 48,000 → 42,000 → €37,000. If the last three months are like the first nine, 2026 will end around €33,000 (−10%). Last year's profit of €6,000 is about €500 a month, or about €2 an hour. The €4,000 savings cover 1.5 months of expenses (about €2,600 a month: 37,000 − 6,000, divided by 12).

Even if +30% worked, it would be about €10,000 of extra turnover, or €2,000–3,000 of profit a year, if 20–30% of each euro stays (my assumption). The strongest force is the town itself: the population has fallen by a quarter, the factory is closed. The marketing of one kiosk will not turn this around.

## The biggest danger

The lease ends around 17 June 2027. An irreversible mistake would be to sign a long term or pay a deposit without knowing where sales are falling.

- Staying too long: if the decline continues (about €3,300 a year), profit would shrink by €650–1,000 a year, but the rent stays; a deposit or a rent increase eats the savings.
- Leaving too early: you lose the income (last year about €500 a month) and a spot you may not get back.

I suggest a red line for the savings of €2,600 (one month of expenses). You are above it, but close. My forecast does not cross it, but a deposit or a rent increase in an extension can.

## What to do

This week:
- Find the lease: rent, deposit, notice period for not extending. I assume 2–3 months (a general assumption, not checked), then the decision must be made by mid-March.
- From the till reports, write out sales by group (press, lottery, other) and by hour for 2024–2026. We will see whether the press is falling (possibly readers are moving to the internet) or everything together, and which hours are empty.

From 26 October for six weeks:
- Check whether parcel locker, post or bill payment service providers would use your kiosk as a point; it can bring regular visitors.
- Offer 10–20 regular customers a reserved newspaper or magazine.

Limit: in total no more than €300 (the savings stay above €3,700) and 3 hours a week out of your 55.

## What to protect and what not to do

My order, from what you will give up last: savings above €2,600; your time and health; the kiosk; +30%. If the kiosk is more important to you than your time, tell me; then we will start with talks about lower rent. Do not: do not sign a long lease before the 31 January data and do not take a loan for stock or advertising.

## Measuring and forecasts

Every Monday: last week's takings. Once a month: the four-week total against the same period last year. Next check on 7 December, decision point on 31 January: if the total is still 10% or more below last year's, the decline is not stopping; ask for a short term with lower rent and prepare an exit plan.

If nothing changes: 2027 about €30,000 (€28,000–32,000). If the plan works: €33,000–36,000, if the town does not keep losing residents fast and the lease does not get worse. Treat +30% as a bonus, not a plan. The forecast will be sharpened most by sales by group, the margin and the rent terms.
