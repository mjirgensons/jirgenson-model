The salon pays its costs and you €1,800, but only €300 a month is left (4% of revenue). The reserve of €2,500 is 0.36 months of expenses, about 11 days. Your pay works out at about €7 an hour.

## The biggest danger

Two weeks with no income, because you fall ill or the groomer leaves. At 60 hours a week that is plausible, but I cannot date it. A two-week shutdown leaves about €2,550 of costs besides your pay, more than the whole reserve (assuming they all keep running). Check whether you have sickness or income cover.

My proposed red line is one month of expenses, €6,900, the least that survives a month without income. You are far below it: the alarm is already ringing. At €300 a month you would be back above it in about 15 months, if no month goes wrong.
