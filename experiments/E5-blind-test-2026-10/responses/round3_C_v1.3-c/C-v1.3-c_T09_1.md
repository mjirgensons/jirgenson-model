In short: +30% (about €43,000) is against the current; right now the biggest decision is the lease, not marketing.

**Where you are**
Turnover is falling: −12.5%, −12%, and the 2026 pace is about €33,000 (−10%; assumption that the months are similar). +30% of that is about €43,000 — about €10,000 more, roughly €27 extra every day. The town has shrunk by a quarter in five years; that is the strongest force, and advertising will most likely only slow it down. Last year €6,000 for about 2,860 hours (55 h a week) is about €2.10 an hour before taxes. The €4,000 savings are about 8 months of what the kiosk earns you.

**The biggest danger**
The end of the lease around June 2027 is the decision point; the real date may be earlier — check in the lease by when you must give notice about renewal. Signing for a long time on the current terms means paying for the spot regardless of takings; in the worst case the kiosk will run at a loss in 2027 and you will work for free. Leaving too early means losing the whole approximately €500 a month and a spot that you may not manage to get back later.

**What to do**
- This week: read the lease and ask the landlord in writing what rent and term they offer, including an option for 6–12 months. Check the contracts with the press distributor and the lottery operator: whether they contain a minimum turnover or opening hours.
- By 15 November: pull the monthly turnover from the till for 2025–2026, separately for press, lottery and the rest; for two weeks write down sales by the hour and close the hours that bring in almost nothing (if the contracts allow).
- By 15 December: two or three tests for no more than €600 in total (15% of the savings), each can be stopped. Ask courier post and ticket or payment service providers about the terms for becoming a parcel or ticket point; offer press delivery to homes or businesses. Keep what brings in money.

**What to protect first**
My suggestion, from what I would give up last to first: the savings; the kiosk income until the lease decision; your time; +30%. Suggested goal for 2027: no less than €33,000. Suggested red line: savings below €3,000 (about 6 months of kiosk income). In the worst case (a loss covered from the savings) it would be reached only at the end of 2027, so today it is not an alarm. If your order is different, tell me — then more could be set aside for the tests.

**What not to do now**
Do not spend the savings on a big campaign, do not sign a lease for several years while you do not know the rent and the test results, and do not buy extra stock “for growth”.

**What to measure and when to check**
Every Monday: the week's turnover against the same week last year and the takings of each test. Once a month: the savings balance. The plan changes if for two months in a row turnover is more than 15% below last year (the trend itself gives about 10%) or the savings slip below €3,000. Next review: on 15 December.

**Two forecasts**
If nothing changes: in 2027 turnover about €30,000 (29,000–31,000), if the town shrinks similarly; profit from −€1,000 (costs do not decrease at all — the worst end) to about €4,900 (costs fall in proportion to turnover).
If the plan works: €33,000–35,000 — my estimate, not a promise. €43,000 would require customers from outside or new services, which your facts do not confirm.
