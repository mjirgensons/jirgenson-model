**On the e-bikes themselves**

The idea may be sound, but I can't judge it without your rental price and expected use. As a rough test, to earn back €12,000 in one season you need about €17,000 of extra rental revenue if the 30% cost applies. Batteries, servicing, insurance and theft will eat some margin anyway. That is about €1,700 per bike. At an example price of €35 a day, that is about 49 rental days per bike, or roughly one day in four of the season. Check this season's records: how often did you turn customers away because you had no bikes left? If often, the case is strong. If rarely, it is weak.
