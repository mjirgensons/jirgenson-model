**Three ways to play it**

**1. Scooter only, on time.** You need an extra €70 by June 1, which is about €18 more a month. That's a bit under half of what you babysit now. Selling stuff you don't use (old games, clothes, tech) could cover it too.

**2. Both by June 1.** You need an extra €150, about €38 a month on top of your €65. That's close to doubling your babysitting, which is tough next to school. It only works if you also get help, like your parents covering the ticket as a birthday or reward present, or splitting it with you.

**3. Concert first, scooter a bit later.** Buy the ticket now (they can sell out or get pricier). Then keep saving: at your current pace the scooter lands around the end of August. If you add that extra €18 a month, it's more like the end of June.

My pick is option 3. The concert has a fixed date and the scooter doesn't, so protect the thing that can't move. If June 1 is a hard deadline for some reason, go with option 1 and skip the concert unless someone gifts you the ticket.

**What to do this week**
- Ask your neighbour if they have more evenings for you.
- Ask your parents if they'd chip in or match what you save. Plenty of parents will.
- Move your €65 somewhere you can't easily spend it as soon as you get it.
- Buy the concert ticket from the €120 you already have (if you go with option 3).

**Costs that aren't in the €450**
- A helmet (roughly €30–50) and a lock (roughly €15–25).
- The rules. Lots of countries have a minimum age, a helmet requirement, or insurance for e-scooters. Check yours before buying, since 15 can be right on the edge.
