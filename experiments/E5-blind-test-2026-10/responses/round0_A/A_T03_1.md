Your baseline first: €18,000 − €15,500 leaves €2,500 a month, a 13.9% margin. Fixed costs are about €11,900, and each member brings in €150 but costs €30 to serve, so every extra member adds €120. Everything below comes from those numbers.

**1. The 20% price cut undoes the rest of the plan.** At €120 a member you keep €90 per member instead of €120. With today's 120 members, revenue falls to €14,400 and you lose about €1,100 a month instead of earning €2,500. That's a €3,600 monthly swing, bigger than all your current profit. You'd need 133 members just to break even, and about 199 (+66%) to reach a 25% margin. Keeping the price, you'd need about 145 (+21%). It also contradicts goal 2: revenue only gets back to €18,000 at 150 members. And the case for it is a guess: nothing says what the new studio charges or that anyone is leaving over price. (I've assumed the cut applies to all members.)

**2. The teachers, second room and app aren't costed.** The plan's only source of money is that €2,500 a month. Each €1,000 of new monthly fixed cost needs about 12 extra members at today's price to still hit 25%, or about 17 at the cut price. Two teachers plus a room plus an app will plausibly add several thousand. Until they have numbers, goal 4 can't be checked. The plan also doesn't say what limits you today: is the room full, are the teachers at capacity? Without that you can't tell whether goals 6 and 7 are needed or premature, and the app isn't tied to anything (retention, bookings, new sign-ups).

**3. The tax arrears have no plan behind them.** €9,000 is €750 a month, 30% of current profit, and "sometime this year" isn't a date. Under the price cut there'd be no profit to pay it from. This is a debt with a real downside (interest, penalties, enforcement; check how it works where you are), yet it sits ninth in a list of eleven, behind growth projects.

**4. Eleven goals, no priority.** Several pull against each other: price cut vs revenue vs margin vs arrears, growing vs working less, more members vs fewer complaints. When they collide, something has to lose, and the plan doesn't say what. Goals 1 and 2 aren't separate goals either: revenue is members × price, so they're the same thing and a means to goal 4.

**5. Several goals can't be judged in December.** "More members", "higher revenue", "better reviews" and "work less" have no number and no starting point, so almost any change counts as success. "Fewer than 3 complaints a month" is a fixed count on a growing base; it gets harder with every member you add, and you haven't said today's number. Goal 4 doesn't say whether 25% is December's month alone or the whole year.

**6. One thing I can't check: your own pay.** If the €15,500 doesn't include a salary for you, the true margin is below 13.9% and 25% is further away. And "work less myself" only makes sense if it's tied to the teacher hires, which the plan doesn't say.

To tighten this, I'd need: the new studio's prices, what the teachers, room and app would cost, whether the room is full, and whether your pay is in the costs.
