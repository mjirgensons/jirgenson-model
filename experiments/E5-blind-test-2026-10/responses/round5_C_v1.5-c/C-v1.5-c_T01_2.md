Short answer: do not order the bikes yet; the winter alone empties your account.

**Where you stand**

A season month leaves €3,300 (14,000 − 4,200 parts − 6,500 fixed). A winter month loses €3,700 (4,000 − 1,200 − 6,500). Over the year that is +€4,600 (23,100 − 18,500), after your own €1,500 a month. The 10 e-bikes at €1,200 each equal 2.6 years of that €4,600.

**The biggest danger**

The coming winter, before any bike. Your account is empty in February and €6,200 short at the end of March. Buying the bikes in March makes the shortfall €18,200.

**What to do**

- Today, you: do not order or pay a deposit. Ask the supplier for delivery time, the price of 5 bikes and payment terms.
- By day 3, you: set your own winter pay (November to March): €750 instead of €1,500 saves €3,750. Check your contract and local rules, then ask your mechanic about shorter winter hours: €1,000 less a month saves €5,000.
- By day 7, you: offer past customers a prepaid spring service booked in winter. If it brings €1,000 extra sales a month, that is €700 after parts, €3,500 over the winter.

Together that is €12,250: March ends at €6,050, just under my proposed red line of €6,500 (one month of fixed costs). Without the extra sales it ends at €2,550.

Bikes: 5 (€6,000) at the end of May and 5 more around the end of July, each only if the balance after paying stays above €6,500. Buying all 10 before the season leaves you at −€5,950 even with these savings. Waiting costs the April and May rentals of the missing bikes; I would wait: it is the cheaper mistake.

**What to protect first (my proposal)**

Last to give up: the shop open, rent, insurance and taxes paid. Before that, your mechanic's hours, then your own winter pay. First to give up: all 10 bikes. Tell me if your order differs. Not now: a deposit, loan or instalment contract for the bikes.

**What to watch**

Every Monday: the account balance. Plan path: €1,250 less each month from November, €6,050 at the end of March. First check on 1 November. If a month-end is more than €1,000 under the path, set your own pay to zero for the rest of winter (another €750 a month) and push the bikes back.

**Two forecasts**

If nothing changes: under €6,500 in December, zero in February, −€6,200 at the end of March, above zero again in May. Worst end: winter sales of €3,000 instead of €4,000 bring zero in January and −€9,700 in March. If the plan works: the path above, five bikes at the end of May. Ranges from your averages, not promises.
