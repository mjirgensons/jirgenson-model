## The numbers

Nobody can honestly guarantee payback or an exact profit; your figures allow this range:

- Revenue €7,000: profit €1,000 a month, payback 39 months (worst end)
- Revenue €9,000: profit €3,000, payback 14 months (middle)
- Revenue €11,000: profit €5,000, payback 9 months

Costs used: €6,000 a month, being rent €1,800, two technicians €3,200 (taken as full cost), and €1,000 for supplies and the rest (my assumption; each €100 off moves profit €100). I am assuming steady revenue of €7,000 to €11,000 (your first salon's €11,000 is my ceiling), the first three months at half. Payback runs from opening and counts the €28,000 fit-out plus €1,500 to €7,500 of opening losses, before tax and interest; at €6,000 revenue or less it never comes. No questions, as you asked; the range narrows most with one technician's monthly sales, a technician's real cost and the fit-out funding.

## Danger and plan

The danger is cash: fit-out plus opening losses needs €29,500 to €35,500. Only €6,200 of your €15,000 reserve is free above the red line I propose (one month of costs, €8,800), so paying from the reserve crosses it at once. The first salon's €2,200 monthly surplus can cover most opening losses if its sales hold when your time is split (a risk to test); even so, at least €21,800 must come from elsewhere.

You, within three days: get written fit-out quotes and count what one technician sells in your first salon. You, before any deposit or hire: get a written funding offer for €21,800 or more, adding any loan instalment to the €6,000. Cheaper option: one technician first (costs €4,400; less capacity).

I propose you give up the second salon first, then the €8,800 reserve floor, and your pay and the first salon last (yours to change). Review when the funding offer arrives, then every Monday: check the reserve and first-salon sales; pause the expansion if the reserve falls below €8,800 or the surplus reaches zero.

If you do not open, the surplus, if left in, lifts the reserve to about €28,200 in six months; if it works, see the €9,000 line.
