- **Today:** do not pay a deposit or sign for the e-bikes yet.
- **This week:** ask your bank for a credit line of about €10,000. It is easier to get while the account is positive and it is season. Ask the supplier for price, delivery date, payment terms (instalments, or payment due March/April) and an insurance quote.

Sizes of the levers, not advice: pausing your own pay in winter = +€7,500; €1,000 more winter revenue a month (service packages, storage) = +€3,500; mechanic hours cut 20% (if his contract allows) = +€3,500.
