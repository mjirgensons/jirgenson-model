- After paying yourself €1,800, the business keeps €300 a month (4% of revenue).
- Your pay works out at roughly €7 an hour: about 260 hours a month (60 × 4.3 weeks).
- Your €2,500 reserve covers about 11 days of expenses.
- No-shows: if 20% of bookings don't turn up and €7,200 comes only from those who do, about €9,000 is booked and €1,800 a month never arrives. That is six times your surplus.

**Biggest risk**

No room for error, with everything resting on you. A 10% revenue dip (-€720) turns €300 profit into a €420 monthly loss; the reserve lasts about 6 months. A 20% dip is -€1,140 a month, about 2 months.

**What to do**

- This week: write a cancellation rule and put it on your booking page, for example a deposit for new clients and past no-shows, and a fee for cancelling under 24 hours. Start a waiting list to refill cancelled slots.
- This month: look at prices as well. A 5% rise is about €360 a month if nobody leaves (an assumption). Aim to build the reserve toward one month of expenses, €6,900.
