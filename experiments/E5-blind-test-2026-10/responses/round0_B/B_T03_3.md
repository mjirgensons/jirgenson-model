**What your numbers show**

You make €2,500 a month, a 13.9% margin. Each member pays €150 and costs about €30 to serve, so each one adds €120 toward your €11,900 of fixed costs. You break even at about 100 members, so you are 20 above that line. A 25% margin at today's price needs about 145 members (25 more).

**Weak spots, most important first**

1. **The price cut (goal 3) breaks the margin goal (4) and the tax goal (9).** At €120 a member, your 120 members bring €14,400 against €15,500 of costs: a loss of €1,100 a month (€13,200 a year). Break-even moves from 100 to 133 members. Keeping today's profit needs 160 members; a 25% margin needs 199 (66% more than now). Even matching today's revenue takes 150 members. It also assumes price is why people choose the new studio, and your plan doesn't say you know that. I assumed the cut applies to all members.

2. **Goals 5, 6 and 7 have no cost in the plan.** I assumed €2,500 to €3,500 a month per teacher, all in, so two teachers add €5,000 to €7,000 a month. At 145 members and €150, that turns a €5,500 profit into +€500 to −€1,500. To keep a 25% margin, every extra €1,000 of monthly fixed cost needs about 12 more members at €150, or 17 at €120. The app, the second room and one-off costs (building the app, fitting out the room) come on top, and you would pay them from a profit of about €2,500 a month.

3. **The tax arrears (goal 9) have no date and no monthly amount.** €9,000 over 12 months is €750 a month, 30% of today's profit. At €120 and 120 members there is no profit to pay it from. Arrears usually grow with interest or penalties, and this is the only goal with an outside party waiting. Where it sits in your list is your call; the cost of waiting is worth knowing.

4. **The goals pull in opposite directions.** Growth (1, 5, 6, 7) takes your time and money, while "work less" (10) and a higher margin (4) need the opposite. Hiring two teachers, launching an app and opening a room in one year is a lot of owner time.

5. **Several goals can't be checked.** "More members" (1), "higher revenue" (2), "work less" (10) and "better reviews" (11) have no number or date. Goal 2 is goal 1 times price, so it isn't separate, and with goal 3 it points the other way. For goal 8, you don't say how many complaints you get now, and a fixed limit of 3 gets harder per member as you grow.
