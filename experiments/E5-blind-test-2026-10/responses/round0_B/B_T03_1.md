**What your numbers show**

You make about €2,500 a month profit on €18,000 revenue, a 13.9% margin (about €30,000 a year). Each member brings €150 and costs about €30 to serve, so each one adds roughly €120 toward your €11,900 of fixed costs.

**Weak spots, most important first**

1. **The 20% price cut works against your other goals.** I assumed it applies to all members. At €120 with today's 120 members, revenue drops to €14,400 and you lose about €1,100 a month. You would need 133 members just to break even, and about 199 to reach a 25% margin. At 150 members (+25%), revenue is back to €18,000 but profit is only about €1,600, less than today. So the cut blocks goals 2, 4 and 9. The plan also doesn't say what the new studio charges or whether anyone has left for it.

2. **A 25% margin by December needs about €2,000 more profit a month, and the plan adds costs.** At €150 and today's fixed costs you would need about 145 members. But the teachers, second room and app all add fixed costs, and none has a number. Each extra €1,000 a month needs about 8 more members just to cover itself, and about 12 more to keep the 25% target.

If "this year" means the current year, only about 2.5 months remain, which is €3,600 a month and more than you earn now. If the price cut leaves you losing money, there is nothing to pay it with. The plan also doesn't say whether interest is running.

5. **Nothing on keeping members.** The plan doesn't say how many leave each month, and "more members" only works if joiners outnumber leavers. "Fewer than 3 complaints" has no starting figure, and more members usually means more complaints.
