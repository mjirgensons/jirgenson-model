**What your numbers show**

In the account €450, in 6 days rent of €1,100 — you are short €650. The credit line covers this: €650 at 12% — about €6.5 in interest a month. Food and the children are provided for by your wife's income. So the rent is a solvable problem, not a catastrophe.

**Main risk**

Not the rent, but that the €3,800 will not come, while the credit line is your only cushion. It will be enough for roughly two rents. And if you keep working for this client, the debt grows.

**Today**

1. Make sure the line works and how many days it takes for the money to reach the account (10 minutes). You can draw it closer to the rent; at least €650, and whether to take more as a reserve is for you to decide.
2. Call the client, don't write. Look for the accounts department or another person, not the one who is silent. Ask: at what stage are the invoices and when is the payment. Right after the call, send a short email: the invoice numbers, €3,800, the payment deadline (for example, 5 working days).

**This week**

- Reread the contract: payment terms, late-payment penalty, the right to suspend work. If new work for the client is under way, whether to suspend it until payment or not is for you to decide.
- They have not paid by the deadline from the email — send an official formal demand.
- Remind other clients about their invoices and look for at least a small order.

**Within the month**

If there is no payment — a lawyer or debt recovery. In most EU countries, for late payment between companies there are a late-payment penalty and a fixed compensation; check the rules of your country. It is worth thinking about how not to depend so heavily on one client, and building up a reserve for the rent.

**Two forecasts** (assumptions: no other income, I am not counting your other expenses)

- If nothing changes: on day 6 you take €650, 0 in the account, €650 of the 2,000 on the line used. In a month the second rent — about €1,750 used, about €250 free. The line will not be enough for the third rent: that is roughly 2 months, and taking your other expenses into account — less. Interest €7–20 a month — the problem is not that but the limit.
- If the plan works: the client pays within 1–3 weeks (no guarantees) — you repay €650 and about €2–5 in interest, roughly €3,100 remains. If they pay in 6–8 weeks — you will repay about €1,775, about €2,000 will remain.

**What to track**

Day 3: whether the client has answered. Day 5: draw the money from the line. The deadline from the email (around day 7): no payment — a formal demand. Day 30: no payment — a lawyer or debt recovery. Come back to me on these days — we will recalculate.
