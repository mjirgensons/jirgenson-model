**What your numbers show**
- Turnover is falling by 10–12% a year: €48 → 42 → 37 thousand. The 2026 figure of €25 thousand in nine months means about €33 thousand for the year (I don't know the December season, so I calculated it simply).
- Since 2023 the decline is about 31%, while the number of residents has fallen by about 25%. Most of it is explained by people leaving, not by bad marketing.
- A profit of €6,000 for roughly 2,900 hours a year is about €2 an hour, or about €500 a month. This year it will be lower, about €4,500–5,000.

**The biggest risk**
The lease ends around June. A new lease is your biggest money commitment, but the income from the kiosk is going down. The €4,000 savings are roughly 8–11 months of the current kiosk income.

The +30% target means about €43 thousand next year. That is more than in 2024, with fewer residents. It is your target, but I consider it luck, not a plan.

**What to do**
- **Today (15 min):** find the lease and write out the end date, the deadline by which you must give notice of extension, and the rent.
- **This week:** write out the turnover by month and by group (newspapers, lottery, other) for the last 12 months. Then you will see what is falling most. Ask the landlord about a shorter lease (1 year), lower rent or an option to get out.
- **This month:** run two cheap tests for 6 weeks, in total up to €500. Compare weekly takings with the same weeks last year. Options:
  - pre-ordering of newspapers or delivery for older people;
  - a message in the local group about opening hours and what's on offer;
  - a service that brings people in, for example parcel pick-up, if your suppliers allow it.

  Which two, decide yourself.

€500 pays off if over the year it brings at least about €2,000 of extra turnover (about €170 a month).

**Two forecasts for 2027** (versus about €33 thousand this year)
- **If nothing changes:** turnover €29–31 thousand, profit about €3,000–4,000. The savings do not grow.
- **If the plan works:** turnover €34–37 thousand (+2 to +11%), profit about €5,000–6,000 before marketing costs. +30% does not fall within this range.

**Assumptions:** each extra euro of turnover gives 25–40 cents of profit, rent and other fixed costs do not change, and the end-of-year season is not taken into account. There are no guarantees.

**What to watch and when to check**
- At the end of each month, compare the turnover with the same month last year. This year the average is about €2,800 a month.
- The big check is at the end of January, when the tests are over and the holidays have passed, and in any case before the notice deadline set in the lease.
- Set your threshold already now: what monthly income you need in order to extend the lease.

**Two questions that would change the recommendations:** how big is the monthly rent and for how long are you being offered the new lease? What percentage of the annual turnover does December usually bring?
